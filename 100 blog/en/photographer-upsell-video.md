## Video Products You Can Offer

### 1. Wedding Trailer ($200-$400)

A 60-90 second cinematic teaser video created from your photos. Delivered within 48 hours of the wedding.

**Why couples love it:** They get something to share on social media immediately, while they wait for the full photo gallery.

**Your effort:** 15-20 minutes of photo selection and AI setup.

### 2. Highlight Video ($400-$800)

A 3-5 minute narrative video covering the wedding day. Your photos, transformed into a cinematic film with music and effects.

**Why couples love it:** It's the [wedding highlight video](/wedding-highlight-video.md) they always wanted, without hiring a separate videographer.

**Your effort:** 30-45 minutes of curation and customization.

### 3. Social Media Cuts ($100-$200)

Short, vertical-format video clips optimized for Instagram Reels and TikTok. Multiple cuts from different moments of the day.

**Why couples love it:** Instant, shareable content that gets likes and comments.

**Your effort:** 10-15 minutes per cut.

### 4. Slideshow Video ($200-$500)

A photo slideshow with cinematic effects, transitions, and music. Classic format, elevated with AI.

**Why couples love it:** It's like a [photo slideshow](/wedding-slideshow-guide.md) from the future — cinematic, emotional, and professional.

**Your effort:** 20-30 minutes.

### 5. Couple Growth Memory Video ($300-$700)

A video tracing both partners from childhood through the wedding day, created from collected family photos.

**Why couples love it:** It's deeply personal and becomes a family heirloom.

**Your effort:** Additional photo collection coordination, 45-60 minutes of setup.

## How to Package and Price Video Services

### Strategy 1: Premium Package Inclusion

Include a basic video deliverable (trailer or short highlight) in your highest-tier photography package. This justifies a higher package price and differentiates your premium offering.

**Example:**
- Essentials Package (photo only): $2,500
- Signature Package (photo + 60-sec trailer): $3,200
- Luxury Package (photo + highlight video + trailer + social cuts): $4,500

### Strategy 2: A La Carte Add-On

Offer video as a standalone add-on that any client can purchase regardless of their photo package.

**Example:**
- Photography packages: $2,000-$4,000
- Video add-on (highlight): +$500
- Video add-on (highlight + trailer): +$750
- Video add-on (full suite): +$1,200

### Strategy 3: Bundle with Album

Create a "Complete Memories" bundle that includes the photo album + video deliverables.

**Example:**
- Photo album: $800
- Highlight video: $500
- Bundle price: $1,100 (save $200)

### Strategy 4: Post-Wedding Upsell

After delivering photos, send a follow-up email offering video creation from the gallery. Many couples who didn't initially purchase video will upgrade after seeing their photos.

**Example email:**
> "Hi [Name]! I hope you're loving your wedding photos. I wanted to let you know that I now offer cinematic video creation from your photos. I can create a 3-minute highlight video that brings your images to life with music and motion. Would you like to see a sample?"
