## What Is a Wedding Photo Slideshow Video?

A wedding photo slideshow video is a video created by compiling your wedding photos into a sequential presentation with transitions, camera movements, music, and visual effects. Unlike a static photo album, it brings your images to life with motion and sound.

The key difference between a modern slideshow and a traditional one? **Cinematic movement**. Instead of photos appearing and disappearing on screen, modern tools add:

- Smooth pan and zoom effects (the "Ken Burns effect")
- Parallax depth (foreground and background moving at different speeds)
- Seamless crossfades and transitions
- Dynamic camera movements that feel like video footage
- Music synchronization that matches visual beats

The result is something that feels less like a slideshow and more like a movie.

Organized chronologically — from getting ready to the last dance. It tells the story of your day in order. This is the most popular format and works great as a [highlight video alternative](/wedding-highlight-video.md).

- "Our Favorite Moments" — curated best-of shots

## How to Create a Wedding Photo Slideshow Video

### Step 1: Curate Your Photos

This is the most important step. Don't dump all 800 photos into the slideshow. Curate ruthlessly.

**How many photos to include:**

| Slideshow Length | Recommended Photos |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| 2-3 minutes | 30-50 photos |
| 4-5 minutes | 60-80 photos |
| 6-8 minutes | 80-120 photos |

**What to prioritize:**
- Emotional moments (vows, tears, laughter)
- Key people (immediate family, wedding party)
- Beautiful portraits and couple shots
- Detail shots that add context (venue, decor, food)
- Candid moments that capture genuine feelings

**What to cut:**
- Duplicate or very similar shots
- Blurry or poorly exposed photos
- Random group shots where you don't know half the people
- Photos that don't contribute to the story

### Step 2: Organize and Sequence

Once you've curated, arrange your photos in a logical order. For a story slideshow:

1. Pre-wedding anticipation (getting ready, details)
2. Ceremony (processional, vows, rings, kiss)
3. Post-ceremony (confetti, portraits, family photos)
4. Reception (entrance, first dance, toasts, cake)
5. Party (dancing, fun moments)
6. Closing (last dance, exit, quiet moment)

### Step 3: Choose Your Music

Music selection is critical. Here are approaches:

**Single Song Approach:**
One song for the entire slideshow. Simple, cohesive, easy to sync. Best for shorter slideshows (2-3 minutes).

**Multi-Song Approach:**
2-3 songs that match different sections. More work to sync, but allows for emotional variety. Best for longer slideshows (5+ minutes).

**Tips for music selection:**
- Choose songs meaningful to you as a couple
- Match the energy arc — slow build, emotional peak, celebration
- Consider instrumental versions for cleaner transitions
- Ensure proper licensing for social media posting

### Step 4: Add Cinematic Effects

This is where modern slideshows diverge from the PowerPoint era. The key effects:

**Pan and Zoom (Ken Burns Effect)**
Slowly panning across a photo or zooming into a detail. This creates the illusion of camera movement in a still image. Essential for every slideshow.

**Parallax Depth**
Separating a photo into foreground and background layers that move at different speeds. Creates a 3D effect that's incredibly cinematic.

**Speed Ramping**
Varying the playback speed — faster during energetic moments, slower during emotional ones.

**Transitions**
Crossfades between photos. Avoid cheesy transitions (star wipes, page curls). Stick to clean, simple transitions.

### Step 5: Add Text and Titles

Optional but effective:
- Opening title with your names and date
- Section titles ("The Ceremony," "The Celebration")
- Meaningful quotes or vow excerpts
- Closing title with a thank-you message

### Step 6: Export and Share

Export in multiple formats for different platforms:
- Full resolution for archiving and TV viewing
- Compressed version for email
- Vertical format for Instagram/TikTok
- Square format for Instagram feed

## Tips for a Better Slideshow Video

### Don't Overdo the Effects

A few well-placed effects are better than constant motion. Let photos breathe. Not every image needs a dramatic zoom.

### Mix Shot Types

Alternate between wide shots, close-ups, portraits, and details. Variety keeps viewers engaged.

### Include Candid Moments

The posed portraits are beautiful, but the candid moments — your partner's reaction seeing you for the first time, your flower girl picking her nose, your grandparents dancing — are what make a slideshow truly special.

### Use High-Resolution Photos

Slideshow effects like pan and zoom work best with high-resolution images. Low-res photos will look blurry when zoomed in.

### Watch It Before Sharing

Always watch the entire slideshow before sharing. Check for:
- Awkward photo sequencing
- Photos that look out of place
- Music volume issues
- Text spelling errors
- Overall pacing
