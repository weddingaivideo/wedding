## Step-by-Step: Creating Your Proposal Memory Video

### Step 1: Organize Your Content

Create a folder and sort your materials chronologically:

1. **Before** — Photos and clips from your relationship, getting ready, traveling to the location
2. **During** — The actual proposal moment
3. **After** — Celebration photos, calling family, engagement selfies

This organization helps the AI (and you) understand the story arc. If you've read our guide on [how AI turns wedding photos into cinematic videos](/blog/ai-photos-to-wedding-video/), you'll know that AI works best when it can identify a narrative structure.

### Step 2: Upload to an AI Video Generator

Upload your organized content to an AI video platform. The AI will:

- **Analyze each photo and video** — Identifying faces, emotions, scenes, and quality
- **Detect key moments** — The knee-drop, the reaction, the embrace, the ring
- **Assess quality** — Flagging any clips that need enhancement
- **Suggest a structure** — Proposing how to arrange your content into a story

### Step 3: Choose a Style

Most AI video generators offer style templates. For a proposal video, consider:

- **Cinematic** — Film-like color grading, dramatic transitions, widescreen format
- **Romantic** — Soft focus, warm tones, gentle transitions
- **Documentary** — More natural, interview-style if you have talking head clips
- **Vintage** — Film grain, retro color palette, nostalgic feel (see our [vintage restoration service](/services/video-enhancement/vintage-restoration/) for more on this aesthetic)
- **Modern** — Clean, minimalist, trendy transitions

Your style choice sets the entire mood. Pick one that matches your relationship personality.

### Step 4: Let the AI Build Your Video

This is where the magic happens. The AI will:

1. **Select the best frames** from each photo/video
2. **Create smooth transitions** between moments
3. **Sync everything to music** — matching cuts to beats
4. **Apply color grading** — consistent, professional look throughout
5. **Add text overlays** — dates, names, quotes (if you want them)
6. **Generate slow-motion sequences** — turning key moments into dramatic slow-mo clips

The whole process typically takes 3-10 minutes, depending on the amount of content and the platform.

### Step 5: Review and Customize

Once the AI generates a draft, you can:

- **Rearrange scenes** — Change the order if the flow doesn't feel right
- **Swap music** — Try different songs until the mood is perfect
- **Adjust pacing** — Speed up or slow down certain sections
- **Add/remove text** — Include captions for context, or let the visuals speak
- **Fine-tune effects** — Adjust color grading, transitions, and filters

Most people spend 15-30 minutes on customization. The AI does 90% of the work; you add the personal touches.

### Step 6: Export and Share

Export your video in the highest quality available. Most platforms offer:

- **4K resolution** — For large screens and future-proofing
- **1080p** — Perfect for social media and sharing
- **Social media formats** — Square for Instagram, vertical for TikTok/Stories

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Don't Over-Edit

The beauty of a proposal video is its authenticity. Don't let AI effects overpower the genuine emotion. A light touch is better than heavy-handed filters and transitions.

### Don't Choose the Wrong Music

Music sets the entire mood. A dramatic orchestral piece might feel over-the-top for a quiet, intimate proposal. A silly pop song might undercut a deeply emotional moment. Match the music to the actual vibe.

### Don't Forget Audio

If you have video footage with audio — the actual words spoken during the proposal — keep it. Don't bury it under music. Those raw, unscripted words are the most valuable part of the video. Many couples choose to have music play softly underneath the real audio rather than replacing it.

### Don't Wait Too Long

Create your proposal video while the memory is fresh. You'll remember details about the moment, the setting, and the emotions that will help you make better creative decisions. Plus, you'll want to share it while the engagement news is still exciting.
