## Before You Start: What You'll Need

### Your Footage Collection

Gather all your video clips into one folder on your computer or phone. Don't worry about organizing them yet — AI will handle that. But do make sure you have:

- **At least 5-10 video clips** (more is better, but quality matters more than quantity)
- **Clips from different moments** — preparation, ceremony, reception, candid shots
- **Reasonable video quality** — most modern smartphones shoot more than good enough footage
- **Total footage of 10-60 minutes** — AI works best with enough material to select the best moments

### Optional but Recommended

- **A song or music preference** — most AI tools have built-in libraries, but your own choice adds a personal touch
- **A few photos** — many AI tools can weave still photos into the video for a richer story
- **A list of "must-include" moments** — if there's a specific speech, dance, or moment you want featured, note it

That's it. No software to install. No tutorials to watch. No technical prerequisites.

## Step 2: Upload Your Footage

### The Upload Process

This is the easiest part. Navigate to the upload section of your chosen platform and:

1. **Select your clips** — drag and drop them into the upload area, or click to browse your files
2. **Wait for upload** — depending on your internet speed and the amount of footage, this takes 1-5 minutes
3. **Let the AI analyze** — once uploaded, the AI scans your footage to detect scenes, faces, emotions, and key moments

### Upload Tips for Best Results

**Do upload in the highest quality available.** If your phone shoots in 4K, upload the 4K files. AI can always downscale, but it can't add detail that isn't there.

**Don't worry about trimming clips.** Upload the full clips, including the messy parts. The AI will identify the best moments and skip the rest.

**Do include variety.** A mix of wide shots, close-ups, candid moments, and planned shots gives the AI more to work with and produces a more dynamic final video.

**Don't upload duplicate clips.** If three guests filmed the same moment from similar angles, pick the best quality version rather than uploading all three.

### File Formats and Compatibility

Most platforms accept common video formats:

- MP4 (most common, universally supported)
- MOV (standard for iPhone footage)
- AVI, MKV, and others are typically supported

If you have footage in an unusual format, most AI tools will attempt to convert it automatically. If that fails, free tools like Handbrake can convert any format to MP4 before uploading.

## Step 3: Choose Your Style and Settings

### Selecting a Video Style

This is where the magic begins. Most AI wedding video platforms offer preset styles that dramatically change the look and feel of your final video:

**Cinematic** — Wide color grading, dramatic transitions, film-like pacing. Think movie trailer vibes.

**Romantic** — Warm tones, soft transitions, gentle pacing. Perfect for intimate moments and emotional storytelling.

**Vintage** — Film grain, muted colors, nostalgic feel. Great for couples who love that analog aesthetic. Explore this further with [vintage restoration services](/services/video-enhancement/vintage-restoration/).

**Energetic** — Fast cuts, bright colors, upbeat pacing. Ideal for reception highlights and party footage.

**Minimalist** — Clean, modern, understated. Lets the footage speak for itself without flashy effects.

**Fun & Playful** — Whimsical transitions, creative effects, lighthearted feel. Perfect for couples who don't take themselves too seriously.

Choose the style that best matches your personality as a couple. You can always create additional versions in different styles later — that's one of the superpowers of AI editing.

### Music Selection

Music makes or breaks a wedding video. Most platforms offer:

- **Built-in licensed music libraries** — curated wedding-appropriate tracks organized by mood and tempo
- **Custom upload** — use your own song (first dance song, "your song," etc.)
- **AI music matching** — some tools analyze your footage's mood and suggest matching tracks

**Pro tip:** Choose your music before finalizing other settings. The AI will sync cuts, transitions, and pacing to the music's rhythm, so the song fundamentally shapes the video's feel.

### Duration and Format Settings

Set your desired output:

- **Duration** — highlight reels typically run 2-5 minutes. For a first video, aim for 3 minutes.
- **Aspect ratio** — 16:9 for standard viewing, 9:16 for Instagram/TikTok Stories, 1:1 for social feeds
- **Resolution** — 1080p is standard; 4K if you want the highest quality and have the storage

## Step 4: Let the AI Work Its Magic

### What Happens Behind the Scenes

Once you've configured your settings, the AI gets to work. Here's what's happening during those processing minutes:

1. **Scene analysis** — The AI identifies every distinct scene in your footage: preparation, ceremony, reception, speeches, dances, candid moments
2. **Moment scoring** — Each moment is scored for emotional intensity, visual quality, and narrative importance
3. **Story construction** — The AI assembles a narrative arc: introduction, building tension, emotional peaks, resolution
4. **Music synchronization** — Cuts, transitions, and pacing are matched to the music's tempo and mood
5. **Color grading** — Footage from different cameras and lighting conditions is unified into a cohesive visual style
6. **Audio balancing** — Background music, ambient sound, and speech audio are mixed for clarity and emotion
7. **Final rendering** — The complete video is rendered in your chosen format and resolution

## Pro Tips for Your First AI Wedding Video

### Tip 1: Start with Your Best Footage

If you have a mix of professional and amateur footage, start with a highlight reel using only the best clips. You can always create a comprehensive version later using all footage.

### Tip 2: Don't Over-Edit

The beauty of AI editing is its restraint. Resist the urge to add too many effects, titles, or transitions. Let the footage and music do the emotional heavy lifting.

### Tip 3: Create Multiple Versions

Your parents want a different video than your college friends. Create tailored versions:

- **Family version:** Longer, includes ceremony highlights, respectful pacing
- **Friends version:** Energetic, reception-heavy, fun moments
- **Social media version:** Short, punchy, optimized for each platform

### Tip 4: Use Photos Strategically

If you have professional photos, sprinkle them into the video at key transition points. A beautiful still photo between video clips can create a powerful pacing effect.

### Tip 5: Trust the AI's Instincts (Then Override If Needed)

AI is remarkably good at identifying emotional peaks and building narrative arcs. Give its first draft a fair chance before making major changes. You might be surprised at moments it highlights that you'd forgotten about.
