### How It Works

The technology combines several AI capabilities:

**Pet Modeling** — Using photos and videos of your pet, the AI creates a detailed 3D model that captures their exact appearance: fur color, markings, size, proportions, and distinctive features.

**Motion Synthesis** — The AI generates natural pet movements based on your pet's breed and behavior. A golden retriever moves differently than a Persian cat, and the AI knows this.

**Scene Integration** — Your pet is placed into your actual wedding footage with correct perspective, lighting, shadows, and scale. The AI analyzes each scene's lighting conditions and matches them precisely.

**Behavior Customization** — Want your dog to sit attentively? Walk calmly? Look at the camera? You can specify the behavior you want, and the AI generates appropriate motion.

**Temporal Consistency** — Your pet maintains a consistent appearance across all scenes, with natural transitions between shots.

## Technical Details: What Makes It Convincing

Creating a believable AI pet in video requires solving several technical challenges simultaneously:

### Lighting Match

Mismatched lighting is the number one giveaway in any composite video. Our AI analyzes:
- Light direction and intensity in each scene
- Color temperature of ambient lighting
- Shadow direction and softness
- Reflective surfaces and how they'd interact with fur

The result is lighting so consistent that the pet looks like they were genuinely filmed in that environment.

### Fur Rendering

Pet fur is incredibly complex to render convincingly. Each breed has different fur length, texture, color patterns, and how it catches light. Our models are trained on extensive pet footage and can reproduce:
- Natural fur flow and movement
- Accurate color gradients and markings
- Realistic light interaction (subsurface scattering on ears and noses)
- Wind and movement effects on fur

### Motion Naturalness

Nothing breaks the illusion faster than unnatural movement. Our motion synthesis creates breed-accurate movements:
- Dogs: natural gait, tail wagging, head tilting, ear movement
- Cats: fluid movement, precise paw placement, tail positioning
- Other pets: species-appropriate behaviors and motion patterns

### Scale and Perspective

Your pet needs to be the right size in every scene and at every camera angle. The AI automatically handles:
- Correct relative scale (a corgi next to a person vs. a great dane)
- Perspective matching for each camera position
- Ground plane alignment (pet actually appears to stand on the floor, not float)
- Depth-of-field matching (pet is appropriately sharp or soft-focused based on scene depth)

## Tips for the Most Convincing Results

### Reference Material Quality

- **Natural lighting photos are best** — Avoid heavily filtered or edited photos as primary references
- **Show distinctive features clearly** — If your dog has a unique marking, make sure it's visible in at least one reference photo
- **Video is more valuable than photos** — Even a short clip gives the AI much more information about how your pet moves

### Scene Selection

- **Wider shots are more forgiving** — Close-up pet integration requires higher reference quality
- **Scenes with clear ground planes work best** — The AI needs to know where the "floor" is to place your pet naturally
- **Avoid extremely busy scenes** — Very crowded, chaotic scenes make it harder to integrate a pet convincingly
- **Consider the narrative** — Think about where a pet would logically be if they were actually at the wedding

### Setting Expectations

AI pet videos are meant to be charming, fun, and emotionally meaningful. They're not meant to deceive anyone. Most couples share them openly, saying "AI put our dog in the video!" — and the response is overwhelmingly positive. People love it.
