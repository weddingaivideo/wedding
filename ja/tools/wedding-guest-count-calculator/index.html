The number nobody knows until it is too late

US planning tools that rank for this question converge on the same figure: 80–85% of invited guests attend a local wedding, meaning 15–20% decline. MySeatPlan, Wedding Notepad and SimplifyCalc all use 80% as the default local acceptance rate and 50–65% for destination weddings. Nearly everyone who RSVPs yes shows up — no-shows are only 2–5% — so the real uncertainty is in the declines.

That gap is expensive. Guest count drives the catering bill, chair and table rentals, the cake, the bar order, favors and the seating chart. Order for the invitation count and you overpay; order for a hopeful low number and you run out of chairs.

Working backwards from a venue

If the room holds 100 and you want it full, you need roughly 118–125 invitations at a local 80–85% acceptance rate — more for a destination wedding. The reverse figure in the results does that math for you. Once the headcount is settled, feed it into the alcohol calculator, the cake calculator and the seating chart maker.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage of invited guests actually attend a wedding?

For a local US wedding, 80–85% is the standard planning figure (15–20% decline). Destination weddings typically see 50–70% attendance depending on travel distance. Close family attend at 95%+, while coworkers and acquaintances may be nearer 70%.

How many invitations should I send for 100 guests?

At an 80% acceptance rate, about 125 invitations. At 85%, about 118. For a destination wedding at 60%, closer to 167. Always leave a 5–10 seat buffer beyond the estimate for late yes responses.

How do plus-ones affect the headcount?

A plus-one roughly doubles that invitation's headcount. If 25% of 150 invited households get a plus-one and 80% accept, that adds about 30 guests. Enter your plus-one share above and the result includes it.

When do I have to give the caterer a final number?

Most US caterers want the firm headcount 7–14 days before the wedding, and it is usually strict. Chair rentals need 2–4 weeks, the cake 2–4 weeks, and the seating chart print waits for final RSVPs.

Should children count as full adult meals?

Many catering planners weight a child's meal at about 0.6 of an adult portion. The result shows both the headcount and a meal-equivalent figure so you can compare quotes fairly.

Does a weekday wedding change attendance?

Yes. Ranked guides put weekday weddings at 20–30% declines versus 10–15% for a local Saturday, because guests need time off work or childcare. Choose the Weekday option in the tool to model it.
