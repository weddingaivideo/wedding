The slice that decides everything

Every ranked cake calculator — Plana, WeddingCakesHub, WedCalc — uses the same industry standard: a 1 inch × 2 inch × 4 inch wedge, roughly the width of two fingers. In the US that is the "dessert portion" served as the main dessert. In the UK the standard is a smaller "finger portion" (1×1 in), which yields about 35% more slices from the same cake. Choose the right one before you order.

Worked examples

100 guests: the classic 3-tier round 12 / 9 / 6 inch combination serves about 100 dessert portions. 150 guests: a 4-tier 14 / 10 / 8 / 6 inch stack serves about 152. With finger portions a 3-tier 12 / 9 / 6 can stretch to 150.

Two traditions that change the math

If you are saving the top tier for your first anniversary — a long-standing UK tradition that has spread to US weddings — add 10–15% to the guest count or order a small separate kitchen cake for that tier's servings. And always tell your baker the portion style you planned around: a generous cut can turn a 3-tier job into a 4-tier one.

Frequently asked questions

How many tiers do I need for 100 wedding guests?

For US dessert-size portions, a classic 3-tier round cake of 12, 9 and 6 inches serves about 100. With smaller UK finger portions, a 2-tier 10/6 cake can cover 100 guests.

What size wedding cake do I need for 150 guests?

A 4-tier round 14 / 10 / 8 / 6 inch combination serves about 152 dessert portions. With finger portions, a 3-tier 12 / 9 / 6 stack will usually cover 150.

What is the difference between dessert and finger portions?

A US dessert portion is about 1×2 inches and is served as the main dessert. A UK finger portion is roughly 1×1 inch — about 30% smaller — so the same cake yields about 35% more slices. It suits weddings where cake follows a dessert course.

Do square cakes serve more people?

Yes, around 25–35% more than a round tier of the same diameter, because every inch can be cut into neat rectangular slices. An 8-inch square gives about 32 finger portions versus 24 for a round.

Should I count the top tier if I am saving it?

No. Exclude its servings from the guest total and add 10–15% to your count, or order a small kitchen cake to supplement. Freezing the top tier for the first anniversary works best with a dense fruit cake.

Can one display cake feed 250 guests?

Not elegantly. Bakers rarely exceed a 16-inch base tier. The standard approach for large weddings is a beautiful 3- or 4-tier display cake plus sheet cake cut in the kitchen and served as slices.
