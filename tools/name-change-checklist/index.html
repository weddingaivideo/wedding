The order matters. Each US agency checks the one before it — start at Social Security or you get sent home to begin again.

Why the sequence is the whole trick

USA.gov, the State Department's passport pages and the ranked name-change guides all describe the same dependency chain: the Social Security Administration's record is what every other agency verifies against. Update your driver's license before SSA has your new name and the records will not match; the passport application then wants the name already on file. Do it out of order and you repeat the appointment.

Documents worth gathering up front

  • Certified marriage certificates (2–3 copies — photocopies are not accepted)
  • Current driver's license or state ID, plus birth certificate for a REAL ID chain
  • Current passport and a new passport photo
  • Form SS-5, and the correct passport form for your situation

The State Department notes that if you can show an ID already issued in your new name, you may not need to submit separate proof of the name change with the passport application — but the DS-11 still asks for the marriage details. Rules differ by state and by how long ago your passport was issued, so treat this list as an organiser, not legal advice.

Not changing your name?

That is now common and requires none of this. Select "Keeping my name" and the checklist narrows to the practical updates: travel bookings, emergency contacts, and making sure your passport and ticket names match exactly. Either way, the other post-wedding task with a deadline is thank-you cards, which US etiquette targets at 4–6 weeks.

Frequently asked questions

What do I change first after getting married?

The Social Security record, immediately after you have certified copies of your marriage certificate. Every other agency — DMV, passport office, banks, employers — verifies your name against SSA's database.

How many certified marriage certificates do I need?

Two to three. Several agencies want their own certified copy and some will not return it. Photocopies and notarised copies are not accepted for the SSA or passport application.

How long does the Social Security name change take?

The new card typically arrives in about 10–14 business days. It carries the same number with your new name, and there is no fee.

Which passport form do I use?

It depends on timing: DS-5504 if the passport was issued less than a year ago and the name changed within that year; DS-82 for a standard renewal by mail; DS-11 if you must apply in person. Check travel.state.gov before sending anything.

Do I have to change my name after marriage?

No. Keeping your birth name is common and requires no government filings. If you travel, just make sure the name on the ticket matches the passport exactly.
