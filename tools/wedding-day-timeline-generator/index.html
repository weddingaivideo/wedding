Why the ceremony time is the anchor

The free timeline generators that rank in the US all work the same way: start at "I do" and build outwards. Bespoke Bride's generator plans hair and makeup chair-by-chair backwards from the ceremony, then runs the reception forwards in a fixed order. WeddingTimeline.org and DigitalRSVPs ship near-identical 29-row templates covering setup, florist arrival, first look, family photos, guest seating, grand entrance, first dance, toasts, cake cutting, open dancing and the send-off.

This tool follows that order and flags the conflicts the ranked tools warn about: photos that push the couple's own entrance late, hair and makeup starting in the middle of the night, or a reception too short for the moments you picked.

Three rules the ranked guides repeat

  • Build in 15-minute buffers. Transitions between ceremony, cocktail hour and reception always take longer than expected.
  • Finish hair and makeup 30 minutes before the photographer arrives. Otherwise the detail shots slip and the whole morning compresses.
  • Send the timeline to the DJ, photographer and venue at least five days out. They will spot conflicts you missed, and one person other than the couple should own the schedule on the day.

If you are adding a sunset portrait slot, look up the sunset time for your venue's town and date — golden hour moves the photo block, not the ceremony. And if you are still months away, start with the month-by-month planning checklist before drilling into the day itself.

Frequently asked questions

What time should hair and makeup start for a 4pm ceremony?

With five people in hair and one stylist plus one makeup artist, most templates start around 9:00am. The generator works backwards from your ceremony time, allowing 45–60 minutes per person and a 30-minute buffer before the photographer arrives.

Should we do a first look?

A first look moves couple portraits before the ceremony and frees the cocktail hour for family photos. Skip it and you will need 45–60 minutes of portraits after the ceremony — which pushes dinner later. Toggle it above and compare.

When should the cake cutting happen?

Most US timelines place cake cutting about 30 minutes after the dance floor opens, following toasts and parent dances. It is also the photographer's cue that the formal coverage is nearly done.

How far in advance should I create the day-of timeline?

Two to four weeks before the wedding, once vendor arrival times and ceremony details are confirmed. Share it with the wedding party and all vendors at least one week before the day.

Can I give this to my vendors?

Yes. Copy result produces plain text you can paste into an email, and Print / PDF gives a clean vendor-ready sheet. Name a point person other than the couple to keep the day on schedule.

What if my reception is at a different venue?

Enable the 20-minute drive option. Travel time eats the cocktail hour, so either extend cocktail or move family photos before the ceremony — the tool shows the collision in the note under the results.
