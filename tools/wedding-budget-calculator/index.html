How to plan a wedding budget

The wedding budget is the single most important number in the entire planning process. It decides the venue, the guest count, the dress, the photographer, the honeymoon — every line item on the 14-category breakdown below. Most couples start with a number pulled out of the air, then discover two-thirds of the way through that the photographer alone costs what they budgeted for the whole wedding. This calculator prevents that. Set the total honestly, distribute the percentages to match your priorities, and watch the pie chart. The number on the right of each row is the cap for that category — go over it only when you have a real quote, not a wish.

Start with the total, not the categories

Most US wedding industry surveys put the 2026 median wedding at a typical figure for ~130 guests. The 14 categories below are weighted toward that median. If your budget is different — a small elopement or a large luxury wedding — change the total first, then drag the sliders. The percentages and pie chart will rescale automatically.

Lock in vendor quotes as you go

The slider is a planning tool, not a contract. When you get a real quote from a venue (for the hall) or a photographer (for 8 hours), override the slider amount with the actual number. The gap between your budget and the actual quote becomes obvious at a glance. The remaining categories stay at the percentages you set; you don't need to rebalance them until you actually have data.

The three big-ticket categories set the tone

Venue, catering, and photography typically account for 60-70% of any wedding budget. Get those right and the rest falls into place. If you need to cut, cut catering (buffet vs. plated, or weeknight vs. Saturday) before you cut the photographer — the photos are what you keep forever, the food is gone in an hour.

Honeymoon is a separate line, not a "misc" line

Put the honeymoon in its own category. If you treat it as miscellaneous, it'll be the first thing you cut when overruns happen elsewhere. The honeymoon is the actual wedding present to yourselves — give it its own line and protect it.

14 wedding budget categories explained

  • Venue & Reception (35-40%) — The biggest line. Includes the ceremony space, reception hall, ceremony + reception fees, and any required insurance.
  • Catering & Bar (25-30%) — Per-head cost times guest count. Buffet, plated and bar service are each costed per head, with bar service on top.
  • Photography (10-12%) — 8 hours of coverage with an album is the standard package; an engagement shoot is booked separately.
  • Videography (5-7%) — Same-day-edit or a 60-90 second highlight film, or full ceremony + reception + editing coverage.
  • Flowers & Decor (8-10%) — Bridal bouquet, centerpieces (one per table), ceremony arch and venue decor.
  • Attire & Beauty (8-10%) — Wedding dress, suit or tux, hair & makeup, and alterations.
  • Music & Entertainment (7-10%) — DJ, live band, ceremony musicians, and sound system rentals.
  • Cake & Desserts (2%) — Wedding cake and dessert table.
  • Rings & Jewelry (4-5%) — Engagement ring + wedding bands together. Excludes the engagement ring if already purchased.
  • Invitations & Stationery (2-3%) — Save-the-dates, invitations, RSVPs, thank-you cards. Or if you use the free digital tools on this site.
  • Favors & Gifts (2-3%) — Guest favors, wedding party gifts and parent gifts.
  • Transportation (2%) — Limo for the couple, guest shuttle and parking.
  • Honeymoon (5-8%) — International 7-10 days, or a domestic 5-7 day trip.
  • Miscellaneous (3-5%) — Tips, marriage license, wedding insurance, postage, alterations, the "we forgot" line.

Frequently asked questions

How does the wedding budget calculator work?

Enter your total wedding budget, pick a currency, and the tool distributes the amount across 14 standard categories using industry-average percentages. Drag any category's slider to adjust its share, or type a custom amount. The pie chart and per-category totals update in real time. Export as CSV when you're done.

What are the average wedding budget percentages?

Industry averages for a US wedding: Venue & Reception 35-40%, Catering & Bar 25-30%, Photography 10-12%, Videography 5-7%, Flowers & Decor 8-10%, Attire & Beauty 8-10%, Music & Entertainment 7-10%, Cake & Desserts 2%, Rings & Jewelry 4-5%, Invitations & Stationery 2-3%, Favors & Gifts 2-3%, Transportation 2%, Honeymoon 5-8%, Miscellaneous 3-5%. These add up to about 100% but can be rebalanced based on priorities.

Which currencies are supported?

USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), JPY (¥), CNY (¥), INR (₹), and KRW (₩). The currency is purely a display label — type the budget in your local currency and the breakdown uses the same symbol throughout.

Is the budget data private?

Yes. All budget values are stored in your browser's localStorage only. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Clear your browser storage to wipe the data.

Can I export the budget?

Yes. Hit Export CSV to download a comma-separated file with all 14 categories, percentages, and amounts. Open in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet. Use Print for a clean PDF-ready layout.
