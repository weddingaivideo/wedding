No. Open any of the 13 tools, use it, close the tab. Nothing follows you. If you opt into the $9.9 AI wedding video flow, you create an account at the order step only — that account is just to deliver your video files.

In your browser. Photos stay on your device (we never receive them, train AI on them, or have a breach risk on our side). Seating chart and budget calculator save to your browser's local storage — clear your browser data and they're gone, so export a CSV when you have something worth keeping.

(3) We have no ads, no affiliate funnels, and no upsell from the free tools.
