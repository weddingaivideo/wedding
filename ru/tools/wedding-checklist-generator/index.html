What a US wedding checklist actually covers

The two pages that dominate US search for this task — The Knot's 12-month planning countdown and Zola's ultimate checklist — use the same spine: 12+ months out, then one section per month, then 2 weeks / week before / day before / day of / after the wedding. Both note that the average engagement is about 15 months, so the first block is research and vision, not bookings.

Our generator follows that structure and adds the one thing a printed PDF cannot: a live progress bar and a saved state in your browser. Tick a task today, come back in three weeks, and it is still ticked. Copy or print the whole list when a planner or parent asks for it.

Where the words come in

Two checklist items are consistently the hardest: writing personal vows and drafting the reception speeches. According to The Knot's 2026 Real Weddings Study of 10,474 couples, 61% of couples now write their own vows — the highest rate recorded — so "start your vows" has moved from a nice-to-have to a standard two-month-out task. Frequently asked questions

How long before the wedding should I start planning?

The Knot's 2026 Real Weddings Study reports an average engagement of 15 months in the US. Twelve months is a comfortable planning window; a small wedding or elopement can be organised in 3–6 months. The checklist adapts to whatever date you enter, keeping the order of tasks intact.

When should wedding invitations be mailed?

US etiquette guidance puts formal invitations at 2–3 months before the wedding, and 4–6 months ahead for a destination wedding. Save-the-dates go out 6–8 months ahead locally and 10–12 months ahead for destination weddings.

Is my checklist saved anywhere?

Only in this browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to a server and there is no account. Clearing browser storage removes the saved ticks — use Print or Copy result first if you want a permanent copy.

Can I print the checklist or share it with my planner?

Yes. Print / PDF produces a clean list without the tool controls, and Copy result gives you plain text you can paste into an email, a shared document, or a planning spreadsheet.

Does this work for a small wedding or elopement?

Yes. Choose Small wedding, Destination, or Elopement and the checklist is filtered to the tasks that still apply — you will not be chased about hotel blocks or a 200-person seating plan.

When should we write our vows?

Both The Knot and Zola place the first vow draft about two months out. Write a loose structure early, then refine it; 61% of US couples now write personal vows, and 150–250 words is the usual target length.
