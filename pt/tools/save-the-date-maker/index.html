What each template is best for

Save-the-date cards work best when they match the wedding's tone. Use this guide to pick a template the first time:

Classic — formal invitations and traditional weddings

Use the Classic template for traditional ceremonies, parent-approved wording, and venues like country clubs, cathedrals, or hotel ballrooms. The champagne palette and serif headline read as "formal" without requiring Latin or curlicues.

Romantic — script fonts and floral motifs

The Romantic template pairs Great Vibes script with a hand-drawn botanical. It fits garden weddings, vineyard ceremonies, and venues with greenery. Couples who want a personal, handwritten feel usually pick this one.

Modern — minimal sans-serif, neutral palette

The Modern template uses a single-color block and clean sans-serif typography. It fits urban weddings, loft venues, art galleries, and couples who already have a strong visual brand.

Photo Forward — engagement photo as the centerpiece

The Photo Forward template places your favorite engagement photo behind a slim white banner. Pick this when you have a strong photo and want the photo to do most of the talking.

Tips for a great save-the-date card

Frequently asked questions

What size is the download?

1080 × 1350 pixels, PNG. That's the Instagram portrait aspect (4:5), which also works for WhatsApp status, Facebook posts, and a 5×7 print at 216 DPI. A 5×7 print at standard 300 DPI needs 1500 × 2100 — for that size, ask us for the higher-resolution export.

Can I use this for a wedding invitation, not just a save-the-date?

Sim – esses modelos funcionam para ambos. Mova a data do casamento para uma linha secundária e adicione informações do local + RSVP se quiser um convite mais completo. Para um longo convite formal, oferramenta de colagem de fotoslida com 2 a 12 fotos, o que é mais próximo de um layout de cartão impresso.

How do I print this at 5×7?

Download the PNG, open it in any editor (Photos app on Mac/Windows, Canva, even Word), choose print at "5×7 in" with "Fit" or "Fill". For best print quality, export at 300 DPI — that's a future enhancement.

Do you store my engagement photo?

No. The photo loads directly into your browser's memory, gets composed into the canvas, and the canvas turns straight into the file you download. Closing the tab clears it. Nothing is sent to any server.
