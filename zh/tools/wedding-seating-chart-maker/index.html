如何规划婚礼座位表

The wedding reception seating chart is the single piece of paper that decides who eats with whom for three hours. Done well, it puts grandparents near the dance floor, keeps college friends at a table of their own, and gives the photographer clean group shots. Done badly, it strands the bride's coworkers at the kids' table. This free planner does the math, the layout, and the export — you make the call on who sits where.

从宾客名单开始，而不是平面图

大多数情侣从数椅子开始。从数椅子开始用户。列出您在婚礼上真正想要的每个人，用一个组（家人、朋友、工作、VIP、其他）标记每个人，并标记加号。您需要的座位总数是每个名字加上每个加一的总和，而不是邀请函上的人数。该工具在右上角统计数据中跟踪此数字：它会更改您添加或删除房客的时间。

组团，然后就座

Group tags are how the auto-assign feature works. When you press Auto-Assign, the tool fills seats table-by-table, putting same-group guests together whenever it can. If you have 32 friends and only 28 friend-seats, the extras spill to a mixed table — and the layout shows you the open seats in red so you can decide. Drag any guest to any seat at any time; the floor plan re-renders in real time.

选择正确的模板

Classic Reception (8 round tables of 8) is the default because it photographs well and works for most 60–80 guest weddings. Long Banquet (3 long tables of 12) is the family-style trend — better for speeches and shared platters. Intimate (5 rounds of 6 + a sweetheart table) is built for elopement receptions under 40. U-Shape with a head table suits formal weddings with a long receiving line.

打印并发布图表

Hit Export PNG to save a 1600×1200 high-resolution image. Print at 11×17 ledger paper for the venue coordinator, 8.5×11 for the hostess station, and shrink-to-fit for the welcome table. The same image is what you text to your wedding planner the morning of — no one has to re-draw a single seat.

制作精美座位表的技巧

  • Seat elderly guests away from the speakers. The DJ booth and dance floor put out 90+ dB. Put grandparents in the perimeter seats furthest from the speakers and nearest the exit.
  • Keep parents near the couple, but not at the head table. The head table is for the couple and the wedding party. Parents get the next-best table — front and center, close to a side door for speeches.
  • Don't seat single people next to other single people. Mix singles into couples' tables. They get more conversation and you'll get more thank-you notes.
  • Dietary restrictions get a tag, not a sentence. Add "V" for vegetarian, "GF" for gluten-free in the dietary note. The caterer reads the chart; the guests don't.
  • Plus-ones count as one seat, not two. If you invited "Sarah +1", that's two seats. The plus-one field on this tool adds a single extra seat, which matches a typical plus-one situation.
  • Make a "must-seat-together" list, not a "must-not-seat" list. Couples, immediate family, and best friends need to be at the same table. Everyone else is flexible. The list of "must-not" is longer than the list of "must" — start with the smaller list.

常见问题

如果我的宾客名单在最后一刻发生变化怎么办？

随时编辑任何客人。将客人拖到不同的座位，或在新添加后再次使用自动分配来重新分配。该工具实时重新渲染。

How many guests can it handle?

Up to 500 guests on a desktop browser. The state is saved in your browser's localStorage so you can close the tab and come back without losing your work. No data is uploaded to any server.

Can I print the seating chart?

Yes. Use Export PNG to save a 1600×1200 high-resolution image, then print at any size. The exported file is 1:1, no watermarks, no signup.

Is the data private?

Yes. All guest data lives in your browser's localStorage. The tool never sends the guest list, names, or table assignments to any server. Clear your browser storage to wipe the data.

What if my guest list changes last-minute?

Edit any guest at any time. Drag a guest to a different seat, or use Auto-Assign again to redistribute after a new addition. The tool re-renders in real time.
