# SERP research — 4 new free browser tools (PDF / contact sheet / GIF / video)

**Search run:** 2026-11-10 · US-intent web search, 10 queries, ~80 result pages read.

**How to read this file.** Everything under a "SERP says" heading is quoted from a page that ranked, with the domain named inline. Everything under a "⇒ Our page should…" heading is **my inference**, not a search result. Numbers are attributed and dated; where two pages disagree I show both and flag the conflict rather than picking one. Nothing here comes from memory — all of it was read off the returned result pages.

**Biggest structural finding (applies to all four queries).** The top 10 for "merge pdf free" / "contact sheet" / "images to gif" / "video converter free" are **not** dominated by iLovePDF, Smallpdf, Adobe, Canva, Kapwing or ezgif. They are dominated by two new species: (1) **browser-local tool pages** (pdfwix, toolbase.cc, thetoolskit.co, convertbox.app, imgtweak, trackmix, freegiftools, clipzeal) whose whole pitch is *"your file never leaves your device"*; (2) **2026 comparison listicles** (fixmypdf.in, pickrack.com, toolpix, clipcraft.ai, thetechbasket, zellahq) that rank the big brands against each other. Both species put the same strings in the H1/description: **free · no watermark · no signup**, and the new ones add **no upload**. That is the entry fee for these queries.

## 5. Cross-cutting conclusions (inference, built on the SERP above)

1. **These four pages compete against browser-local tool sites, not against Adobe.** The old brands rank only inside 2026 comparison listicles. Our pages must lead with "runs in your browser / nothing is uploaded" or they lose to a page that says it.
2. **Three strings are the entry fee:** `free` · `no watermark` · `no signup`. Add `no upload` as the fourth — the strongest and newest differentiator.
3. **Every ranking tool page publishes its limits.** A hidden limit reads as a scam in this SERP; a stated limit ("up to 500 MB, limited by your device's RAM") reads as honest. Use a stated ceiling over a silent one.
4. **Dates matter.** The SERP is full of 2026-dated listicles (fixmypdf, pickrack, toolpix, clipcraft, thetechbasket, zellahq; pixeltools checked 2026-11-04). Any competitor limit we quote should carry "checked on <date>".
5. **FAQ blocks are mandatory and literal** — 6–12 questions per page, phrased the way the SERP phrases them, answered in 2–4 sentences. The questions are listed verbatim in §1–§4; copy the wording, not our paraphrase.
6. **"People also ask" is a second content target** per page; the strongest items are non-obvious ("How to Make a Contact Sheet in the Darkroom", "My bank PDF is password protected — what is the password?", "How to make Chrome Homepage a GIF").
7. **No star ratings and no SOC2 badges appeared anywhere.** Trust here is built from retention promises, standards citations (ISO 32000, ISO 216, ANSI), named engines, published limits and honest limitation statements. We can match all five with no badge.
