Wedding Photo Collage Guide

A collage reads as one image. Consistent gaps, a limited number of frames and a shared crop style matter more than the arrangement itself.

ตรวจทานล่าสุด · 2026-11-15

เปิดเครื่องมือฟรี →

ข้อมูลอ้างอิงอย่างรวดเร็ว

Print ratio4:3, 3:2 and square are the safest print shapes
FramesThree to seven images reads better than twenty
SpacingUse one gap value everywhere, scaled to the canvas
CropKeep the same aspect ratio for every cell

วิธีใช้งาน

Decide the final output size before placing photos. For a framed print, match a standard frame ratio and leave a small outer margin. Then crop every source image to the same cell shape so faces are treated consistently, and let one image be the anchor — usually a portrait at roughly twice the area of the others.

ความแตกต่างระหว่างภูมิภาค

Common print sizes differ by region: the US uses inch sizes such as 8 × 10 and 11 × 14, while Europe uses A4, A3 and 20 × 30 cm. If you are ordering a canvas or a photo book spread, use the lab's exact pixel template rather than a generic ratio. For social sharing, a 4:5 portrait or 9:16 vertical fills more screen space than a square.

ข้อผิดพลาดที่ควรหลีกเลี่ยง

Avoid mixing landscape and portrait crops inside an unadjusted grid, which produces awkward empty strips. Keep the collage out of the bleed area, and do not place a face exactly on a seam. If the collage will be printed, export at 300 dpi and check it at actual size on paper — small faces that look fine on a phone disappear in print.

คำตอบอย่างรวดเร็ว

How many photos should a collage have?

Three to seven is a reliable range. Beyond that, each frame becomes too small to read, especially in print.

What size should I export for print?

300 dpi at the final dimensions, for example about 2400 × 3000 pixels for an 8 × 10 inch print.

Should all photos have the same crop?

Yes. Consistent cell shape and spacing are what make a collage look designed rather than assembled.
