Wedding Gift Amount Guide

There is no obligation to cover your plate. The amount depends on how close you are, your own budget, and what is customary where the wedding takes place.

ตรวจทานล่าสุด · 2026-11-15

เปิดเครื่องมือฟรี →

ข้อมูลอ้างอิงอย่างรวดเร็ว

Close familyUsually the most generous tier, often a substantial cash or registry gift
Close friendsCommonly a mid-range gift, sometimes shared with a group
ColleaguesOften a modest group gift or a small registry item
Rule of thumbYour relationship and budget matter more than the meal cost

วิธีใช้งาน

Start with what you can comfortably afford, then adjust for closeness, whether you are bringing a guest, and whether you have already paid for travel and accommodation. A group gift lets several people give something meaningful without each overspending. If there is a registry, using it is genuinely helpful to the couple.

ความแตกต่างระหว่างภูมิภาค

In the US registries are standard and cash gifts are common; some couples use a honeymoon fund. In the UK and much of Europe cash or a cheque is frequent, and a registry may be a small list or absent. In many Asian wedding cultures money is given in a ceremonial envelope at the event, with amounts sometimes chosen for auspicious numbers — follow the family's custom rather than a Western rule of thumb.

ข้อผิดพลาดที่ควรหลีกเลี่ยง

Do not calculate a gift from an estimated per-head catering cost; that figure is private and varies by venue. Do not bring a large physical gift to the reception — the couple has to transport it. And send it reasonably close to the wedding or within a few months after, with a note so it can be acknowledged properly.

คำตอบอย่างรวดเร็ว

Should a gift cover the cost of my meal?

No. That is a guideline some guests use as a floor, not an obligation. Your relationship and your own budget are the right starting points.

Is cash acceptable?

In most places yes, and in many cultures it is the norm. Use the couple's preferred method if they have indicated one.

How late can I send a gift?

Within a few months of the wedding is generally fine. Include a note with your full name so the thank-you card is addressed correctly.
