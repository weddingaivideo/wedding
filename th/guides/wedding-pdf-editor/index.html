Wedding PDF Editing Guide

Most wedding PDF problems are invisible on screen: missing fonts, mixed page sizes and images that were downsampled during export.

ตรวจทานล่าสุด · 2026-11-15

เปิดเครื่องมือฟรี →

ข้อมูลอ้างอิงอย่างรวดเร็ว

Page sizeMatch the lab's template exactly, in mm or inches
FontsEmbed all fonts or convert text to outlines
BleedAdd 3 mm or 1/8 inch if any artwork reaches the edge
ColoursRGB for consumer labs; ask before converting to CMYK

วิธีใช้งาน

Decide the destination before you build the file. A PDF for a printer needs a fixed page size, bleed and embedded fonts; a PDF for guests to read on a phone only needs small file size and selectable text. Merge related documents into one ordered file, then check every page for consistent orientation and margins before exporting.

ความแตกต่างระหว่างภูมิภาค

The US uses inch-based sizes such as 5 × 7 and 8.5 × 11; Europe and most of Asia use A-series sizes such as A4 and A5. Sending an A4 design to a US lab — or letter to a European one — produces unexpected cropping. Some professional presses request PDF/X with CMYK and a specific colour profile, while consumer photo labs prefer sRGB JPEG or a simple PDF. Always read the lab's spec first.

ข้อผิดพลาดที่ควรหลีกเลี่ยง

Do not flatten a PDF that guests need to fill in, and do not 'print to PDF' from a browser without checking margins and headers. Watch for a font that looks right on your machine but substitutes elsewhere. If the file contains personal details such as addresses or a guest list, remove metadata and consider a password before sharing publicly.

คำตอบอย่างรวดเร็ว

Why did the printer crop my design?

The page size or bleed did not match the lab's template. Export at their exact dimensions and keep content inside the safe margin.

Should I embed fonts?

Yes for any print PDF. Embedding, or converting text to outlines, prevents substitution on the printer's system.

How do I make a PDF smaller?

Downsample images to the target resolution, remove unused pages and metadata, and use the PDF's built-in optimiser. Do this on a copy, not the master.
