Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to restore an old wedding video?

Standard turnaround is 48–72 hours. For complex restorations (heavy damage, multiple tapes), it may take 5–7 business days. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee.

What video formats do you accept?

We accept VHS, VHS-C, DV, Hi8, MiniDVD, 8mm, 16mm film, and all modern digital formats (MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV).

How do I send my video files?

After submitting your request, we'll send you a secure upload link. Files are encrypted and deleted 30 days after delivery.

Can you restore a video with missing audio?

Yes. Our AI can reconstruct audio from visual cues and ambient sound.

What's your refund policy?

If you're not satisfied with the result, we'll revise it twice at no extra cost. Full refund is available if we can't meet your requirements after revisions.

How do you handle my personal footage and privacy?

Your footage is stored encrypted on secure US-based servers. Files are automatically deleted 30 days after delivery. You can request earlier deletion at any time by emailing privacy@weddingai.video. We never share your footage with third parties without written consent.

What about AI safety and bias in your processing?

We use commercial-grade AI models with safety guardrails. A human filmmaker reviews every output before delivery. We do not generate deepfakes of real people.

Who owns the final video and can I use it commercially?

You own the final video. We retain a non-exclusive license to use anonymized excerpts in our portfolio unless you explicitly opt out in writing. You may use the video for personal, social media, and non-commercial purposes. Commercial use (e.g., selling as part of a wedding planning business) requires written permission.

Do you offer rush delivery?

Yes. Most services offer 48-hour rush delivery for an additional 30-50% fee, depending on the service. Check the service page or mention it in your quote request and we will confirm availability.

How does pricing work for multiple services?

Bundle discounts apply when you book 2+ services on the same project: 10% off for 2 services, 15% off for 3 services, 20% off for 5+ services. Discounts are applied automatically. The first-customer discount (mention "FIRST" in your request) gives an extra 20% off your first service, stacking up to a 30% maximum.
