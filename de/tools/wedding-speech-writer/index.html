The speech everyone remembers — for better or worse

US and UK guides that rank for this topic agree on the running order: the father of the bride or a parent welcomes, the couple may speak, and the best man speaks last. They also agree on length — The Perfect Wedding puts a short toast at 300–750 words / two to five minutes, while UK guides allow five to eight minutes for a best man. Longer than that and the room goes.

The structure is remarkably consistent across the ranked pages: an opening that earns attention, two or three specific stories about one person, a turn toward the couple, a genuine compliment, and a clear toast that tells the room to raise its glass.

What to include, and what to cut

DoAvoid
Introduce yourself and say how you know the personOpening with a dictionary definition of love
Tell 1–2 true, specific stories with a place and a detailInside jokes only two people understand
Give the partner a genuine complimentExes, dating-app stories, stag-do secrets
End on a clear "please raise your glasses"Trailing off with "so… yeah, cheers"
Practise aloud three times; use note cardsReading a phone screen, or winging it after the open bar

A useful test from one NYC officiant: if it has no "I will" lines and is addressed to the room, it is a toast, not a vow. If you are writing the vows themselves, use the vow writer instead — the two forms are easy to confuse and do different jobs.

Timing it

The live word count above uses 130 words per minute, a comfortable pace for a nervous speaker with pauses for laughter. Build in two to three seconds after each joke — the silence is where the laugh lands. If your draft runs two minutes in practice, expect closer to two and a half on the night.

After the wedding

Speeches are one of the few parts of the day captured in the couple's own voices. If you are recording them, keep that audio — it is often what turns a wedding film from a slideshow into a story. Later, the same guest list drives the thank-you notes.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a best man speech be?

Two to five minutes is the most common US guidance (300–750 words); some UK guides allow five to eight. Shorter is almost never a mistake. Practise aloud and time it — a draft that runs two minutes in practice usually takes two and a half live.

What is the correct order of wedding speeches?

Traditionally a parent of the couple welcomes guests, the couple may thank everyone, then the maid of honour, then the best man last. Modern weddings reorder freely; confirm the running order with the couple and the DJ or MC.

What should a wedding speech never include?

Exes, dating-app stories, stag or hen weekend secrets, jokes at the partner's expense, and inside jokes the room will not understand. Every ranked guide also warns against opening with a dictionary definition of love.

Do I have to be funny?

No. Warm and specific beats a stand-up routine. The most reliable structure is one funny story, one sincere story, a genuine compliment to the couple, and a clear final toast.

Should I read from my phone or from cards?

Note cards. Several ranked guides call this out explicitly: a phone reads as a script, distracts the room, and is awkward to hold with a glass. Use Print / PDF and cut the page to card size.

Is anything I type sent to a server?

No. The speech is assembled in your browser from the words you type. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and the draft is saved only in this device's local storage until you clear it.
