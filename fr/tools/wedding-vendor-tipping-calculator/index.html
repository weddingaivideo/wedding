The trap that costs couples hundreds

Every ranked 2026 tipping guide leads with the same warning: a "service charge" on a catering or venue contract (often 18–24%) is not necessarily a tip. It may be a house fee that never reaches the staff. Ask the manager directly whether it is distributed as gratuity. If it is, you are covered; if it is not, tip the servers and bartenders directly. Never pay a separate gratuity line twice.

What couples actually did in 2026

Zola's 2026 Wedding Spend Survey asked 720 couples within six months of their weddings who they tipped: caterers 73%, hair and makeup 71%, photographers 70%, DJ or band 67%, and planners 48%. Zola suggests budgeting roughly 5–10% of the total wedding cost for tips. Most couples hand out cash on the day, so the practical job is working out the total before the venue day — not after.

How to hand it over

A week before the wedding, withdraw the cash and put each amount in a labelled envelope with the vendor's name. Ask a trusted person — best man, maid of honour or coordinator — to distribute them as each vendor wraps up. A short thank-you note inside each envelope goes a long way. Use Print / PDF for the envelope list. If a vendor owns the business, a tip is appreciated but not expected; a five-star review and referrals are worth more. This is customary US guidance — tipping expectations differ across Europe, so check local norms for a destination wedding.

Frequently asked questions

Do I tip if there is already a service charge?

Not necessarily. A service charge of 18–24% is often a house fee, not a gratuity. Ask the catering or venue manager whether it is distributed to staff. If it is, you are covered; if it is not, tip the staff directly — but never pay a separate gratuity line twice.

How much should I tip a wedding photographer?

A flat $50–$200 is customary, and it is optional — many photographers own the business and set their own price. Tip second shooters and assistants too; a five-star review and referrals are often worth more than cash.

Do you tip the wedding DJ?

Yes, 10–15% of the fee or a flat $50–$150 for a solo DJ. For a band, $25–$50 per musician, or one envelope to the bandleader who splits it. Hand it over at the end of the reception.

How much do most couples spend on wedding tips in total?

Ranked guides put a mid-size US wedding between roughly $500 and $1,000+, with some 2026 budget models reaching $1,500–$4,000 for a 100-guest wedding with a full vendor list. Zola suggests budgeting 5–10% of the total wedding cost.

Should I tip a vendor who owns the business?

A tip is appreciated but not expected — they set their own price. Focus cash tips on staff and employees. A heartfelt review, referrals, or a thank-you note are excellent alternatives.

When and how are tips handed out?

In cash, on the wedding day. Prepare labelled envelopes a week ahead and ask a trusted person — a coordinator, best man or maid of honour — to distribute them as vendors finish. Print the envelope list from this page so nothing is forgotten.
