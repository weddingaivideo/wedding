The post-wedding task nobody budgets time for

Zola's free Instant Thank Yous, TheWeddingPlanner.ai and Text My Wedding all exist for the same reason: a 100-guest wedding means 100+ individually worded notes. The ranked tools converge on the same recipe — greet by name, name the specific gift, say how you will use it, add one personal memory, close warmly, and send within 4–6 weeks.

Batch mode, and why the notes must differ

Paste one guest per line as Name | gift | what it is for and the tool writes a different note for each row — the detail ranked tools call out as the difference between 140 notes and one note printed 140 times. Two cousins comparing cards at Thanksgiving should not find identical sentences.

Handwritten, typed or sent by message?

Zola notes that digital thank-you notes are now widely accepted, and Australian/US guides agree a warm message beats a late formal card. A short, timely note always reads better than a long, late one. If you are writing by hand, use Print / PDF for the drafts and keep them at the kitchen table with a pen.

Once the notes are done, the other post-wedding job with an order dependency is the name change sequence. And the guest list you just worked through is the same list that feeds a wedding film share — the moment most couples finally send it out.

Frequently asked questions

How soon should wedding thank-you cards be sent?

US and Australian etiquette guidance targets four to six weeks after the wedding; three months is the outer limit most guests notice. A short, timely note always reads better than a long, late one.

What do I write for a cash gift?

Thank the generosity without naming the amount, then say what it is going toward — the honeymoon, a first home, or savings. A specific purpose turns a polite line into a warm one.

Do I have to send a note to someone who did not give a gift?

Yes if they attended. Focus entirely on their presence and a shared memory from the day, and never mention that no gift was received.

Can I send thank-you notes by email or message?

Yes. Zola and other ranked guides say digital notes are now widely accepted, especially for close friends. Handwritten cards still carry more weight for older relatives and formal families.

How do I stop 140 notes sounding identical?

Use batch mode: one guest per line with their own gift and purpose, and each note is written from its own inputs. Vary the tone between warm, formal, short and playful for different groups.

Is anything I type uploaded?

No. Names, gifts and notes stay in this browser. There is no account, no server call and no AI model — the wording is assembled from templates you control.
