How to pick the right one

  1. Shortlist 3-5 favorites from the list above.
  2. Search each on Instagram, TikTok, and X. If someone's already using it (especially a 2024-2026 wedding), drop it — your guests will see their photos.
  3. Say it out loud 4-5 times. If it trips your tongue, your guests won't type it either.
  4. Pick a runner-up. Couples often pick one primary tag and a secondary "kid-friendly" tag for grandparents who use the wrong one half the time.

Frequently asked questions

Is this generator AI?

It runs in your browser using combinatorial templates — no AI call, no upload, no rate limit. For each pair of names, it returns the same set of patterns filtered by the style chips you toggle. That's why this tool can stay 100% free with no signup, no email, no watermark. If you want a longer, more bespoke generation with an LLM, that's a paid service we don't currently offer.

Can I suggest tags that aren't on the list?

Yes. After you pick a favorite, combine it with a year, location, or inside joke to make it yours — the generator does the formula work, you do the personality. Wedding hashtags usually read better when one human touch is added.

How long should a wedding hashtag be?

Under 20 characters is ideal. Up to 30 is acceptable. Over 30 is hard to type on a phone, hard to remember after a champagne toast, and easy to misspell. The generator warns you with a red badge on any tag over 30.

Should I include the year?

نعم إذا كانت تركيبة اسمك شائعة (Smiths، Garcias، Nguyens). العام ينشر الاصطدامات على Instagram. تخطي السنة إذا كانت أسماءك غير عادية - أضف أرقامًا إليها#إيما تجتمع مع ليامفقط يجعل الكتابة أكثر صعوبة.

What about weddings outside the US?

The generator works in any language that uses Latin script because it only operates on first/last name spellings. For Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, Thai, Hindi, etc., the patterns still work — type the romanized spelling you want guests to see.
