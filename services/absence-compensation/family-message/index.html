How Remote Family Messages Work

  1. Coordinate with Your Family — Send a simple recording link to family members who can't attend. They record on their phone — no app, no special equipment. We provide instructions in plain English and 12 other languages.
  2. AI Lip-Sync & Framing — Our AI adjusts each recording for lighting, framing, and lip-sync to the moment where they'll appear. Aunt María's kitchen recording looks like she was always there, with consistent visual quality throughout the film.
  3. Choose the Moment — Tell us where the messages should appear — during the ceremony, at the reception entrance, or as part of a pre-ceremony highlight reel. We can place 5-15 messages in a single 3-5 minute video.
  4. Seamless Integration — Messages are woven into the natural flow of the day. Transitions match the surrounding footage so each message feels like a guest who is physically present, then steps away to make room for the next speaker.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my family isn't tech-savvy?

We designed the recording process to be dead simple: click a link, allow camera access, record, submit. If your 80-year-old grandmother can record a video on her phone, she can do this. We provide a printable PDF guide and a live phone-support number.

How many family members can record messages?

There is no hard limit. We've done weddings with 4 messages and weddings with 40+ from family across 6 continents. Each message typically runs 15-45 seconds.

Will the messages disrupt the ceremony flow?

No. The messages are pre-recorded and edited into the video in post-production. During the live ceremony, your day continues naturally. The messages become a special feature of the final film you watch together afterward.

How do remote family members record their messages?

We send each contributor a private link by email. They record on their phone, laptop, or tablet — whatever they have. We give them a 5-minute tip sheet so they know to film landscape, in a quiet spot, and speak directly to camera.

What if relatives have different devices or speak different languages?

It doesn't matter. We accept any video format and any language. Our editors can also add subtitles in any language and, if you choose the multilingual service, even dub the audio.

How long does a family message video take?

Once all family members have submitted their messages (typically 1–2 weeks), final editing and seamless embedding takes 5–7 business days. We can accept last-minute submissions up to 48 hours before the wedding for a small rush fee.
