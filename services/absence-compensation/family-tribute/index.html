A Note on Sensitivity

We understand this is an emotional subject. Every tribute we create for a deceased loved one is handled with the utmost care and respect. We work closely with you to ensure the memorial video honors your loved one exactly as you'd want. We never use AI to fabricate statements or put words in anyone's mouth. Instead, we focus on visual and audio elements that feel authentic, dignified, and in loving memory of the person you lost.

A real story — with permission to share

Carolina’s mom was supposed to be at the wedding. She was two months too early.

When Mamá passed, Carolina thought about not having a wedding at all. Her mom had been her biggest cheerleader — the person who’d been saving wedding magazine clippings since Carolina was twelve.

Carolina sent us a single 1985 wedding photo of her parents — Mamá in a white dress, Papá laughing at something off-camera, the original print slightly curled at the edges. We activated it: subtle motion, a gentle blink, a soft turn of the head, a look toward camera as if she’d just heard the music start.

The clip played during a quiet moment of the reception. Carolina says: “My whole table burst into tears. She was there, in the most beautiful way. Thank you for giving me that moment with her again.”

This kind of work is the most important thing we do. We never use it lightly, and we work closely with you to make sure the result honors exactly what you want to remember.

How We Create a Lasting Tribute

  1. Share Photos & Recordings — Send us scanned photos, home videos, voicemails, or any recordings of your loved one. Even 1-2 photos are enough — we can work with very little source material to create a meaningful segment.
  2. Choose the Moment — Tell us where in your ceremony or reception the tribute should appear — usually right after the vows, before the first dance, or at a quiet moment during dinner. We work with your photographer or videographer to integrate the tribute seamlessly.
  3. AI Restoration & Animation — We restore old photos to high resolution, animate subtle movements (a smile, a wave) where appropriate, and synchronize any available audio. The tone is always respectful and dignified — never uncanny or gimmicky.
  4. Final Integration — The 30-90 second tribute is edited into your final wedding film with tasteful music and transitions. It feels like a natural part of the celebration, not a separate insert.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this service culturally appropriate for all traditions?

Yes. We've created tributes for Christian, Jewish, Hindu, Buddhist, Muslim, and secular ceremonies. We adapt the tone, music choice, and placement to honor your family's specific traditions and beliefs.

What if I only have very old, low-quality photos?

We specialize in restoration of degraded material. Our pipeline includes AI upscaling, scratch removal, and colorization (optional). Photos from the 1950s-1980s are our most common input and produce surprisingly emotional results.

Can I review the tribute before the final video is delivered?

Absolutely. We send a preview of the tribute segment for your approval before integrating it into the main video. You can request changes to music, length, or pacing at no extra cost.

Will the AI voice really sound like my loved one?

Only if you have prior recordings of their voice (voicemails, home videos, voicememos). If you have at least 30 seconds of clean audio, our model can extract their vocal patterns. Without prior audio, we will not fabricate statements or put words in anyone's mouth — we focus on visual elements like photo activation instead.

How do you make sure the tribute feels respectful, not creepy?

Every project gets a senior editor who specializes in memorial content. We work closely with you to approve the pace, the music, the framing, and any voiceover words. You can request as many preview rounds as you need before the final delivery.

How long does a family tribute take to produce?

Standard delivery is 7–10 business days, because every tribute is human-reviewed twice for tone, accuracy, and respect. Rush delivery in 5 days is available for an additional 30% fee. We never skip the human review — it is non-negotiable for this service.

What happens to my loved one's photos and likeness after delivery?

All source materials and the final tribute are stored on encrypted, US-based servers and permanently deleted 30 days after you confirm delivery. We never share them, never train on them, and never use them for marketing without your written permission. You can request immediate deletion at any time, even before delivery.
