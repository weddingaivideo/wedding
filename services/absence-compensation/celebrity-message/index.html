How Our AI Celebrity Messages Work

  1. Pick an Archetype — Choose from 4 personality templates: charming leading man, glamorous leading lady, witty comedian, or smooth talk-show host. Each has its own voice, posture, and delivery style.
  2. Send Your Details — Provide names, dates, the inside jokes, and any anecdotes you want included. The more personal, the more believable. We craft a script that sounds like it was written by someone who actually knows you.
  3. AI Generation & Voice — Our AI generates a 60-second video with realistic voice, lip-sync, and gestures. The result looks like a real celebrity filmed a personal message in their living room — not a stock template.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the actual name of a real celebrity?

No. Our service uses AI-generated archetypes inspired by the style of public figures, but never impersonates real people. This protects you legally and ethically, while still giving the 'A-list surprise' effect. The result feels like a celebrity filmed it, without misrepresenting anyone.

Will my guests be disappointed if it's not a real person?

Almost never. The surprise is in the production quality and personal content. We've had 90% of viewers genuinely believe it was a real message from a famous person — the AI is that good. Reveal at your own pace.

Can I use this for a non-wedding event?

Yes. We create AI messages for milestone birthdays (40th, 50th, 60th), anniversaries, retirements, and corporate events. Pricing is the same — it's a one-time creative service, not a wedding package.

Can I customize the celebrity's message?

Yes. You provide the script (or we help you draft one) and pick the personality style — warm, funny, dramatic, or heartfelt. We don't impersonate any specific real person; instead we generate a voice and style that fits the vibe you're going for.

How do you handle legal / impersonation concerns?

We don't replicate any specific real person's likeness, voice, or persona. Our output is an original AI voice with a chosen tone. You retain full usage rights to the final video, including for paid social posts.

How long does a celebrity-style message take to produce?

Standard delivery is 5–7 business days, which includes 2 rounds of revisions so the message lands perfectly for the moment. Rush delivery in 3 days is available for an additional 30% fee. We will never cut the revision rounds — your guests should not see a half-finished opener.
