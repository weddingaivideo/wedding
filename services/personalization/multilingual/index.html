How Multilingual Wedding Videos Work

  1. Upload Your Original — Send us your ceremony or reception video in any language. We work with 20+ languages including English, Spanish, Mandarin, Hindi, Arabic, Portuguese, French, Bengali, Russian, Japanese, Korean, and more.
  2. Pick Target Languages — Tell us which languages you need. A typical Indian wedding in the US might need English + Hindi + Tamil. A multi-cultural European wedding might need English + French + German + Spanish.
  3. AI Voice & Subtitles — Our AI generates a natural-sounding voiceover in each target language, lip-synced to the original speaker's mouth movements. We also generate subtitle tracks in SRT format for hard-of-hearing guests or those who prefer to read.
  4. Final Delivery — Get separate dubbed tracks (so viewers can choose) plus a single combined version with all languages mixed appropriately. Subtitles are burned-in or as a toggleable file, your choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the AI voice sound robotic?

No. We use a state-of-the-art multilingual voice model trained on native speakers. The voice has natural intonation, breathing, and emotional range. Most viewers don't realize it's AI until you tell them.

Can you handle regional accents (e.g. Mexican Spanish vs. Spain Spanish)?

Yes. We have voice models for major regional variants of each language. Just tell us which region your family is from, and we'll match the accent and vocabulary accordingly.

What if my ceremony has 4+ languages?

We can handle up to 5 simultaneous languages with proper volume mixing. For ceremonies with more, we recommend subtitles-only for the additional languages to keep the audio clear.

Which languages do you support?

20+ languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Arabic, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Vietnamese, Thai, Tagalog, Dutch, Greek, Hebrew, and more. If your language isn't on the list, ask — we can usually add it within a week.

Do you do live translation during the wedding?

Not for the live event itself (that's a different kind of service). We translate the final edited video into the target language with subtitles, voiceover, or both. Most couples also order a separate AI-translated version of the trailer for the reception screen.

How long does multilingual video production take?

Standard delivery is 5–7 business days for a single language. Each additional language adds 1–2 business days. Rush delivery in 3 days is available for an additional 30% fee.
