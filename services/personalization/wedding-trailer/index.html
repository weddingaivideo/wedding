How We Create Cinematic Trailers

  1. Upload Raw Footage — Send us your full ceremony, speeches, and reception videos. Unedited original files from your videographer or guests' phones all work. We accept 4K ProRes, MP4, MOV, and most camera formats.
  2. AI Curates the Highlights — Our model watches the full footage and picks the 30-90 best moments — emotional peaks, laughs, glances, key reactions. It factors in shot composition, audio quality, and storytelling rhythm.
  3. Editor Polishes — A human editor assembles the selected moments into a trailer with pacing, color grading, and licensed music. The AI does the heavy lifting; the editor adds the human touch — pacing, transitions, that final emotional beat.
  4. Multiple Formats Delivered — Get your trailer in 16:9 for TV/YouTube, 9:16 vertical for Instagram Reels/TikTok, and 1:1 for Facebook. One source video, every platform covered.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the final trailer?

Three options: 30 seconds (Instagram-ready), 1 minute (TikTok/YouTube Shorts), or 3 minutes (cinematic YouTube premiere). Most couples order all three. Pricing is per minute, not per format.

Can I pick the music?

Yes. We have a library of 500+ licensed cinematic tracks across genres (orchestral, indie, pop, classical). You can also supply your own song if you have the rights. We sync cuts precisely to the beat.

Do you use the same music as the original wedding videographer?

Not unless they specifically license it. Wedding videographers typically hold sync rights to their music selections. We use fresh, properly licensed tracks from our own library to avoid any copyright issues on your social channels.

What music licensing do you use?

We use royalty-free cinematic music from Artlist, Musicbed, and Epidemic Sound. You also have the option to bring your own song (we'll need the licensing proof) or to use a popular track through our preferred-licensing partner for a small additional fee.

How long until I see my trailer?

3–5 business days for standard delivery. Rush delivery (2 business days) is available for an additional fee. Each round of revisions takes 1–2 business days.
