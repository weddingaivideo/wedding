How the AI Video Generator Works

  1. Upload 20-100 Photos — Mix of formal portraits, candid shots, and detail close-ups. Group photos, candids, and solo portraits all work. The more variety, the richer the final video.
  2. Write a Brief Prompt — Describe the mood in 1-2 sentences: 'emotional cinematic with a focus on our first look and vows, warm color grading, instrumental soundtrack.' You can also leave it blank and let our AI pick the style.
  3. AI Generation — Our model composes a 3-5 minute cinematic film — complete with camera movements, color grading, and music sync. Each shot is generated for 3-8 seconds with natural motion interpolation between frames.
  4. Review & Refine — Get a first cut in 3-5 days. Request changes to music, pacing, shot selection, or color. Two revision rounds are included. Final delivery in 4K MP4, optimized for both TV viewing and social sharing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many photos do I need?

Minimum 20, maximum 200. The sweet spot is 40-80. Below 20, the AI has limited material; above 100, the video can feel rushed unless we extend the runtime.

Will it look AI-generated or real?

Our current model is specifically trained on cinematic wedding footage, not generic stock. The result looks indistinguishable from a professionally edited film. Most viewers don't realize it was AI-generated until you tell them.

What if I have both photos and raw video?

We can integrate both. Send everything; we'll pick the best moments from each. Many couples use raw video for the main moments (vows, first dance) and photos for the in-between scenes (guest reactions, detail shots).

What if I don't have a videographer? Will photos work?

Yes — most of our customers use only photos. We work with 20–100+ photos depending on the package. The more photos, the longer the final film. Phone snapshots work; we up-scale and color-match for consistency.

How long does AI video generation take?

Standard delivery is 3–5 business days. Rush delivery (2 business days) is available for an additional fee. You'll get a draft for review, and 2 revision rounds are included.
