Three output styles, one goal

Every GIF or clip we make falls into one of three styles. You can mix and match across a single order, or pick a single style for the whole pack — most couples go with a mix that covers both reaction GIFs and vertical short-form video.

Reaction GIFs (3–5 sec loops)

The classic format. Small, looping, and built for the group chat. We pair these with witty captions where the moment calls for it. Best used for the moments where facial expression carries the joke — the "I do," the cake cutting, the best man's open mouth.

Meme Templates (caption-driven)

A still or short clip with a typed caption — the format you see on Twitter, Reddit, and Instagram meme accounts. We write the captions in your voice, or you provide a list of inside-joke captions from your guest book. Best for: the dance floor, the slow songs, the group toast.

Vertical Short Clips (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

9:16 vertical video, 5–15 seconds, with optional trending audio. These are the format that gets reach on social media. Best for: the proposal moment, the first look, the exit at the end of the night. The kind of clips your friends will share and tag you in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my guests see these and be embarrassed?

We carefully review every clip before delivery. Anything potentially embarrassing (drunk uncle, wardrobe malfunction) is flagged or removed. The tone is wholesome fun, not roast. You can also request a private review and reject any you don't like.

Can I use this for a TikTok account I want to start?

Yes — many couples use our service as a content library for their new TikTok or Instagram account. The vertical format and trending audio integration make posting effortless. Some couples have gained 10K+ followers from a single meme pack.

Do the GIFs loop smoothly?

Yes. Our AI detects the natural start and end of an action (a smile, a laugh, a hug) and loops the clip at that boundary. The result is a seamless loop with no visible jump cut, optimized for the platform's autoplay behavior.

Who owns the memes?

You do. Once delivered, you can use the GIFs anywhere — family group chat, Instagram, TikTok, Slack, wedding thank-you cards, printed wedding albums. No watermarks, no licensing fees, no expiration.

Can I share them on social media right away?

Yes. We deliver in standard GIF format and short MP4 loops, both ready for direct upload to Instagram Stories, TikTok, iMessage, and WhatsApp. Each clip is under 10 seconds for fast loading.

How long does meme generation take?

Most packages are delivered in 2–3 business days. Larger sets (20+ GIFs) take 3–5 business days. Same-day rush delivery is available for an additional 50% fee.
