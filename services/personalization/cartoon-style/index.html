How We Apply Animation Styles

  1. Choose Your Style — Pick from 8 animation styles: Ghibli (soft, hand-drawn), Pixar (3D, vibrant), anime (cel-shaded), watercolor (painted, dreamy), pencil sketch, oil painting, comic book, and storybook.
  2. Send Source Footage — Upload your original ceremony or reception video. The AI needs visual reference for faces, clothing, and the environment. 10+ minutes of source footage gives the best consistency.
  3. Style Transfer — Our AI processes each frame to apply the chosen style while preserving facial features, expressions, and motion. The result looks like a real animated film, not a cheap filter.
  4. Add Music & Deliver — Choose a soundtrack to match the style (orchestral for Ghibli, jazz for Pixar, etc.). Final delivery in 4K MP4. Optional vertical 9:16 cut for social media.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my guests' faces be recognizable in the animated version?

Yes. The style transfer preserves facial features, expressions, and identity. The animation is the look, not a different person. Friends and family will recognize themselves immediately — just with an artistic twist.

Can I combine styles? Like Ghibli for the ceremony and Pixar for the reception?

Absolutely. Many couples do exactly this — softer Ghibli for the emotional ceremony, more vibrant Pixar for the party reception. The style can change at any moment in the timeline.

What if I want to use a real animation studio instead of AI?

That's a different budget tier ($20,000-$100,000+ for a 5-minute fully custom animation). Our AI service gives you 80% of the look at 1% of the cost. The other 20% is the part where you didn't actually need Pixar to animate your vows.

Which style looks most like us?

Ghibli and Pixar styles lean soft and romantic. Anime and Comic are bolder. Watercolor and Pencil feel classic and timeless. We can apply a different style to the ceremony vs. the reception if you want — e.g. Ghibli for the ceremony, Comic for the reception dance.

Will the cartoon look like us, or a generic animated couple?

Our pipeline preserves facial structure, hair, glasses, and distinguishing features. The result reads as a stylized version of you, not a stock illustration. We send a 15-second sample first so you can approve the style before we process the full video.

How long does cartoon-style rendering take?

Standard delivery is 5–7 business days for a 3–5 minute video. Longer films take 7–10 business days. Rush delivery in 3 days is available for an additional 30% fee.
