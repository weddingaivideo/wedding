Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of pets work?

Dogs, cats, rabbits, parrots, horses — we've done them all. For exotic or very large animals, ask us; some species need custom motion models.

Do you train a model on my pet, or use a generic one?

We use a generic pet-motion model tuned with your pet's photos (we ask for 5–10 clear reference shots). The result preserves your pet's coloring, size, and distinguishing features — it reads as your pet, not a generic golden retriever.

Does my pet need to "act"?

Not at all. We use your existing photos. Our AI generates realistic pet movements and wedding scenarios — no pet acting required.

Can I include my actual wedding footage?

Yes. If you have clips of your pet from your real wedding, we can integrate them with AI-generated elements for a mixed reality video.

How long does the pet wedding video take?

Standard turnaround is 5–7 business days. Rush delivery (48h) is available for an additional 50%.
