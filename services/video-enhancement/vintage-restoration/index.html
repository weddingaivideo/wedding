Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to restore an old wedding video?

Standard turnaround is 48–72 hours. For complex restorations (heavy damage, multiple tapes), it may take 5–7 business days. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee.

Can you restore a video with missing audio?

Yes. Our AI can reconstruct audio from visual cues and ambient sound.

What tape and film formats do you accept?

We accept VHS, S-VHS, VHS-C, Hi8, Digital8, MiniDV, Betamax, MiniDVD, 8mm film, Super 8, 16mm, and all modern digital formats (MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV). For physical media, ship to us insured and we ship it back after digitization.

My tape is so damaged it won't play. Can you still recover it?

In most cases, yes. Our team uses professional VCRs with built-in TBC (time-base correction) and AI frame reconstruction. Tapes with broken housing, wrinkled tape, or mold can usually be salvaged. We do a free diagnostic first and only charge if we can deliver a usable result.

Will upscaling to 4K make my old footage look fake?

No. Our AI upscaler is specifically trained on archival wedding footage. It adds detail and sharpness without inventing features that weren't there. The result looks like a higher-quality version of your original — not a plastic AI hallucination.

Can you restore just a clip, or do I need to send the whole tape?

Just a clip works. Send us the timestamp range or describe the moment (e.g., "the first dance, around 18:30"), and we'll restore only that section. This is cheaper than full-tape restoration. If you're not sure which moments matter, we can review the full tape and quote per-segment.
