Why AI Slow Motion Is Different

Traditional slow motion requires shooting at high frame rates (120fps, 240fps) — which most wedding cameras don't do. Our AI slow motion takes standard 24fps or 30fps footage and generates new intermediate frames between each real frame using advanced frame interpolation, creating fluid, natural-looking slow motion without the robotic artifacts of basic interpolation. How AI Slow Motion Works

  1. Upload Your Footage — Send us your raw ceremony, first-dance, or reception clips. Any resolution from 24fps to 60fps works — common formats like MP4, MOV, and ProRes are all supported.
  2. Frame Interpolation — Our AI analyzes motion between consecutive frames and generates intermediate frames using optical-flow algorithms. The result is smooth motion without the stutter or ghosting that simple frame-doubling produces.
  3. Choose Your Speed — Pick the target frame rate — 60fps for cinematic feel, 120fps for sports-style drama, or 240fps for the buttery-smooth magazine look. Each speed is tuned separately for natural results.
  4. Receive Final Video — Get your enhanced video in 3-5 business days. The original audio is preserved; only the video frames are regenerated. Optional delivery in vertical 9:16 for social media reels.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my footage is only 24fps or 30fps?

No problem. We specialize in up-converting standard frame rates. Most wedding videos are 24-30fps from consumer cameras, and our AI handles these gracefully. The result looks like it was shot on a high-end cinema camera.

Will slow motion look fake or laggy?

Our model is trained on real cinematic footage, not synthetic data. The interpolation preserves the original motion vectors, so a dancer's dress sway or a candle flame looks physically accurate — no AI-generated artifacts.

How long does processing take?

Standard turnaround is 3-5 business days for clips up to 10 minutes. Longer videos (e.g. a full 1-hour ceremony) take 7-10 days. Rush delivery in 48 hours is available for an additional 30% fee.

What's the maximum frame rate you can output?

Up to 240fps. For most wedding moments, 120fps hits the sweet spot between cinematic and natural. 240fps is reserved for very short, dramatic moments like the bouquet toss or cake smash.

Do you also upscale resolution, or just frame rate?

We can do both as a bundled service. Resolution upscaling (1080p to 4K, etc.) is quoted separately based on source quality and target resolution. Most couples start with frame-rate-only.
