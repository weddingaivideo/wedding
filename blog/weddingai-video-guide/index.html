The 12 Styles, Explained

  1. Cinematic: Hollywood-style color grading + slow-motion cuts + orchestral music. The most popular style (40% of orders).
  2. Documentary: Handheld-style candid coverage + interview cuts + acoustic music. Best for full-event capture.
  3. Traditional: Classic ceremony structure + formal cuts + traditional music. Suits religious or formal weddings.
  4. Storytelling: Narrative arc from preparation to reception + voiceover-friendly + emotional music. Suits love-story reels.
  5. Vintage: Film-grain + retro color grade + period music. Suits heritage weddings or 90s/2000s nostalgia.
  6. Slow-motion: 2-8x frame interpolation + emotional highlights + ambient music. Best for ceremony highlights and first dance.
  7. Trailer: 60-second cinematic teaser + dramatic music + hook-driven cuts. Suits save-the-date and social sharing.
  8. Cartoon (8 sub-styles): Ghibli / Pixar / anime / comic / watercolor / sketch / claymation / storybook. Suits quirky couples and guest gifts.
  9. Multilingual: Subtitles + AI voice dubbing in 11 languages. Suits multicultural weddings with international family.
  10. Pet feature: Dogs/cats/horses in ceremony + pet-narrative cuts. Suits pet-loving couples.
  11. Meme / comedy: Blooper reel + viral hooks + IG-ready cuts. Suits social-media-shy couples who want humor.
  12. Family tribute: Deceased parent voice reconstruction + photo restoration + caption in any language. Suits grieving couples.

Who Should Use WeddingAI.video

WeddingAI.video is for couples who want cinematic wedding videos at 1% of the cost of a traditional videographer. It's not for: complex multi-cam live events, drone footage, or live-streamed ceremonies — those still need traditional videographers.
