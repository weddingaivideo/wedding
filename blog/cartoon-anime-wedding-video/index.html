How to Choose Your Style

  1. Match your venue mood: Ghibli suits outdoor garden or forest weddings; Pixar suits ballroom or hotel; anime suits urban or themed weddings.
  2. Consider print pairing: If you have illustration-style wedding invitations, pair with the same cartoon style for visual consistency.
  3. Test with one photo first: Most styles process in 9 minutes — try a single favorite photo before generating the full film.
  4. Add music + captions: AI auto-suggests music; you can swap to any of 11,000+ tracks or upload your own.
  5. Combine with realistic versions: Many couples order 2 versions: a realistic highlight reel + a cartoon version for sharing on IG Reels / TikTok.

Beyond the Cartoon Look: Real Wedding Uses

Cartoon-style wedding videos work beautifully for save-the-date announcements, engagement announcement reels, anniversary videos (especially 5th / 10th / 25th), and guest gifts. For the main ceremony film, most couples still want a realistic version alongside the cartoon.
