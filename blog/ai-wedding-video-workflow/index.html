AI Wedding Video Workflow: Zero-Effort Method (Photos In, Film Out)

Follow the complete AI wedding video production flow from photo upload to final export. Step-by-step guide to creating your wedding film with AI in 2026.

1 min read

Traditional wedding videography requires filming on-site (1 videographer, $1,500-$5,000), multi-week editing (Premiere Pro, color grading, audio mix), then delivery via USB or cloud. Total: 8-16 weeks per couple. AI inverts this: photos in, film out, 9 minutes per style.

The 5-Step AI Wedding Video Workflow

  1. Step 1: Upload 30+ Photos: Drop your 30-50 wedding photos into our secure browser-side uploader. No app install, no account required. Files stay on your device unless you opt to share them with us.
  2. Step 2: Pick a Style (12 Options): Choose from 12 wedding video styles: cinematic, documentary, traditional, storytelling, vintage, Ghibli anime, cartoon, meme, slow-motion, trailer, multilingual, or family tribute. Each style costs $9.9.
  3. Step 3: AI Generates in 9 Minutes: Our AI processes your photos + style choice + optional music (11,000+ tracks available) and outputs a 60-90s finished video. Most styles complete in under 9 minutes.
  4. Step 4: Preview + Optional Revision: Preview your video in-browser. If you'd like changes (music swap, caption edit, color tone), request 1 free revision with 24h turnaround.
  5. Step 5: Download or Share: Download MP4 (1080p) or share via private link. Full commercial rights included. Files encrypted and deleted after 30 days unless you request longer retention.

When the Old Workflow Still Wins

For complex multi-cam live events (full ceremony + reception with 2+ cameras, drone footage, livestream), traditional videography is still required. AI cannot capture in-the-moment audio or live reactions. Use AI for highlight reels, trailers, save-the-dates, family tributes, restoration, and multilingual edits.
