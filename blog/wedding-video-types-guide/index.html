How to Choose the Right Style for Your Wedding

  1. Start with your venue: Outdoor garden / forest suits Ghibli, vintage. Ballroom / hotel suits cinematic. Beach suits slow-motion. Urban / industrial suits anime.
  2. Match your personality: Quirky couples: meme + cartoon. Romantic: cinematic + trailer. Multilingual family: multilingual + traditional. Pet owners: pet + cinematic.
  3. Consider your audience: Older family prefers traditional / documentary. Younger IG-sharing friends prefer trailer + meme.
  4. Try multiple styles: Since each style is $9.9, many couples order 3-4 styles: realistic highlight + trailer + one niche (cartoon or meme).
  5. Bundle discounts: 2 = 10% off, 3 = 15%, 5+ = 20%: Mention "BUNDLE" in your quote request and we apply automatically.

Beyond the Standard 5 Styles

If you fit the standard 5-style mold (cinematic / documentary / traditional / storytelling / vintage) and want a single realistic highlight reel, any competent videographer delivers. AI shines when you want niche styles (cartoon, multilingual, pet) or fast 9-min turnaround.

How to Choose the Right Video Type for Your Wedding

With 12+ types, choosing can feel overwhelming. After producing 9,400+ wedding videos across all types, we've learned that 90% of couples end up ordering 2-3 types, not just one. Here's the framework for picking the right combination.

The most common bundle: one for the ceremony itself, one for social media, and one for the legacy keepsake. Each serves a different audience and timeline, and together they cost less than a single traditional videographer.

  • For the ceremony itself: Full ceremony video (60-90 min) or highlight reel (3-5 min) for sharing. Skip trailer — trailers work for the reception, not the ceremony.
  • For social media: Trailer (60-90s) or vertical 9:16 Reels version. IG/TikTok favor short, emotional, captioned.
  • For absent family: Multilingual dubbing or subtitles. Most-requested languages: Spanish, Hindi, Mandarin, Arabic.
  • For legacy keepsake: Full documentary edit (10-30 min) on USB drive. This is what your kids will watch in 30 years.
  • For fun / shareable: Meme-style blooper reel. Surprisingly popular for the 18-32 age group.
