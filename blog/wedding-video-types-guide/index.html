30 Types of Wedding Videos Every Couple Should Know (2026 Guide)

Explore 30 types of wedding videos — from highlight reels to AI-generated trailers. Find the perfect video style for your 2026 wedding with this complete guide.

12 min read

When most people think of a "wedding video," they imagine one thing: a long recording of the entire wedding day. But wedding videos have evolved dramatically. In 2026, there are dozens of distinct types of wedding videos, each serving a different purpose, telling a different story, and appealing to a different audience.

Whether you want a cinematic highlight reel, a fun social media clip, a heartfelt family tribute, or something completely unique, there's a wedding video type that's perfect for you. This guide covers 30 of them — what they are, when to use them, and how to create them.

Classic Wedding Video Types

1. Full-Length Documentary

The traditional wedding video: a complete, chronological recording of the entire wedding day, from getting ready to the send-off. Typically 60-120 minutes long. This is the "raw footage" version that captures everything.

**Best for:** Couples who want to relive every moment, family archives.

2. Wedding Highlight Reel

A condensed, cinematic version of the day, featuring only the best moments set to music. Typically 3-10 minutes. This is the most popular type of wedding video and the one most couples share on social media.

**Best for:** Social media, sharing with friends, anniversary viewing. See our wedding highlight video guide for detailed instructions.

3. Wedding Trailer

A short, teaser-style video (1-3 minutes) that builds anticipation for the full video. Often released before the full video to generate excitement. Cinematic, dramatic, and emotionally charged.

**Best for:** Social media announcements, building excitement. Our wedding trailer guide covers this in detail.

4. Same-Day Edit (SDE)

A highlight video created and shown during the wedding reception itself. The videographer films the ceremony and afternoon, then quickly edits a highlight reel to play at the reception. Incredibly impactful but requires skilled editing under pressure.

**Best for:** Wowing reception guests, creating a shared emotional moment.

5. Wedding Short Film

A narrative-driven video that tells the couple's love story through interviews, footage, photos, and creative editing. More artistic and personal than a standard highlight reel. Typically 5-15 minutes.

**Best for:** Couples who want a meaningful, story-driven keepsake.

Pre-Wedding Video Types

6. Save-the-Date Video

A short, shareable video announcing the wedding date. Often features the couple, the date, and a brief message. 15-60 seconds.

**Best for:** Digital announcements, social media.

7. Wedding Invitation Video

A video version of the traditional wedding invitation, including all essential details (date, time, location, RSVP). Can be formal or casual depending on the wedding style. Our wedding invitation video guide covers this thoroughly.

**Best for:** Modern couples, eco-conscious couples, destination weddings.

8. Wedding Countdown Video

A series of short videos counting down to the wedding day. Released at regular intervals (daily, weekly, or monthly) to build excitement.

**Best for:** Social media content, building anticipation. See our wedding countdown video guide.

9. Engagement Video

A video documenting the engagement — either the proposal itself or a post-engagement celebration. Often used as an engagement announcement.

**Best for:** Sharing the engagement news, social media announcements. Our proposal video ideas offer 20 creative approaches.

10. Pre-Wedding Photoshoot Video

A cinematic video captured during a pre-wedding or engagement photoshoot. Often features the couple in scenic locations, set to music.

**Best for:** Save-the-dates, wedding website content, reception display.

Ceremony Video Types

11. Full Ceremony Recording

An unedited recording of the entire ceremony. Captures every word, every moment, every reaction. Priceless for family members who couldn't attend.

**Best for:** Family archives, absent family members.

12. Ceremony Highlight

A condensed version of the ceremony, featuring the processional, key moments (vows, rings, kiss), and recessional. Typically 3-5 minutes.

**Best for:** Sharing the ceremony without the full length.

13. Vow Video

A close-focus video of just the vow exchange. Intimate, emotional, and deeply personal. Can be combined with a family tribute video for a powerful emotional experience.

**Best for:** Anniversary viewing, intimate keepsake.

Reception Video Types

14. Speech Video

A dedicated video of toasts and speeches. Captures the words, emotions, and reactions. Can be edited to combine multiple speeches into one video.

**Best for:** Preserving meaningful words, sharing with those who couldn't attend.

15. First Dance Video

A dedicated video of the first dance, often with slow-motion enhancement. Can include preparation footage and guest reactions.

**Best for:** Social media, anniversary viewing. Our slow-motion enhancement service can make this extra special.

16. Party/Dancing Video

An energetic, music-driven video of the dance floor. Fast cuts, upbeat music, and lots of energy. Captures the fun and celebration of the reception.

**Best for:** Social media, showing the fun side of the wedding.

Social Media Video Types

17. Instagram Reel/TikTok Edit

A short (15-90 seconds), trendy edit optimized for social media. Features quick cuts, popular audio, text overlays, and platform-specific formatting.

**Best for:** Viral sharing, reaching a wide audience.

18. Instagram Story Series

A series of short clips (15 seconds each) designed for Instagram Stories. Can be released throughout the wedding day as a live narrative.

**Best for:** Real-time sharing, day-of content.

19. Wedding Meme Video

A fun, humorous video that plays on wedding tropes, inside jokes, or trending formats. Not every wedding video needs to be serious — sometimes funny is better.

**Best for:** Entertaining guests, social media virality. Our meme generator service can help create these.

20. Reaction Video

A video capturing reactions to the wedding video — parents watching for the first time, friends seeing the proposal, grandparents reliving the ceremony. These reactions become part of the story.

**Best for:** Emotional impact, family sharing.

Special Purpose Video Types

21. Family Tribute Video

A video honoring family members — those present and those who have passed. Combines family photos, old videos, and wedding footage to celebrate the families that shaped the couple.

**Best for:** Rehearsal dinners, emotional reception moments. Our family tribute service specializes in this.

22. Absent Family Message Video

A video compilation of messages from family members who couldn't attend the wedding. They record their well-wishes in advance, and the video is played at the reception.

**Best for:** Destination weddings, elderly family members, military families. See our family message service.

23. Celebrity Message Video

A personalized video message from a celebrity (or celebrity impersonator) congratulating the couple. A fun surprise for the reception.

**Best for:** Entertainment, surprise moments. Our celebrity message service offers this.

24. Guest Book Video

Instead of a traditional written guest book, guests record short video messages. These are compiled into a guest book video that captures voices, faces, and personalities.

**Best for:** Interactive reception activity, personalized keepsake.

25. Pet Wedding Video

A video featuring the couple's pets in wedding-themed settings. Can be a standalone video or incorporated into the main wedding video.

**Best for:** Pet lovers, social media content. Our pet wedding service creates adorable pet wedding videos.

Creative and Artistic Video Types

26. Cartoon/Animated Wedding Video

A cartoon-style recreation of the couple's love story or wedding day. Fun, unique, and completely different from traditional wedding video.

**Best for:** Unique keepsakes, reception entertainment. Check out our cartoon style service.

27. Vintage Film Style

A wedding video edited to look like it was shot on vintage film — with grain, color shifts, and retro effects. Nostalgic and romantic.

**Best for:** Couples who love vintage aesthetics. Our vintage restoration service can create this look.

28. Drone Cinematic Video

Aerial footage of the venue, landscape, and outdoor moments. Creates dramatic, sweeping visuals that ground-level cameras can't achieve.

**Best for:** Destination weddings, outdoor venues, scenic locations. See our destination wedding video guide.

29. Multilingual Wedding Video

A wedding video with subtitles or narration in multiple languages. Essential for multicultural weddings where guests speak different languages.

**Best for:** International couples, multicultural families. Our multilingual service handles this perfectly.

30. Photo Slideshow Video

A video created entirely from wedding photos — no video footage required. Photos are enhanced with transitions, effects, music, and text to create a dynamic, engaging video.

**Best for:** Couples with great photos but no video footage. Our wedding slideshow guide covers this in detail.

Choosing the Right Types for Your Wedding

You don't need to create all 30 types. Here's a recommended combination:

The Essential Package

  • Highlight reel (for sharing)
  • Full ceremony recording (for archiving)
  • Invitation video (for announcements)

The Complete Package

  • Everything in Essential, plus:
  • Wedding trailer (for building excitement)
  • Speech video (for preserving words)
  • Family tribute (for emotional impact)

The Social Media Package

  • Everything in Complete, plus:
  • Instagram Reels (for viral sharing)
  • TikTok edits (for younger audiences)
  • Countdown videos (for building anticipation)
