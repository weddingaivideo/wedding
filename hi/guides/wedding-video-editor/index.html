Wedding Video Editing Guide

Edit to the emotional arc, not the chronology. A three-minute film that opens on a vow and closes on the dance floor beats a strict timeline.

Last reviewed · 2026-11-15

Open the free tool →

Quick reference

LengthThree to five minutes suits a highlight film
ClipsKeep most shots 2–5 seconds; hold a speech or vow longer
AudioClean ceremony audio carries the whole film
Export1080p H.264 MP4, AAC audio, 16:9 unless vertical is required

How it works

Build three passes: select the clips, assemble to music, then fix sound and colour. Lay down the best audio first — vows, a reading, a speech — and cut pictures over it. Keep transitions simple; straight cuts and a few dissolves age far better than effects. Finish with a colour pass so indoor and outdoor footage sit together, and always watch the whole film once at normal speed before exporting.

Regional differences

Music licensing differs sharply by use: a track cleared for a personal wedding video is not automatically cleared for a photographer's portfolio or a paid advertisement, and platform detection varies by country. If the film will be posted publicly, use a licensed library track. Frame rate also depends on region — 24 or 25 fps is common where PAL traditions apply, 30 or 60 fps in North America — so match the source footage rather than forcing a conversion.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not export a single master for every channel: a vertical social cut needs its own reframing, and a large ProRes master is useless to relatives on a phone. Avoid heavy noise reduction on low-light reception footage, which turns faces waxy. Back up the project file and the source media together, and keep the audio stems separate so a music change does not mean a full re-edit.

Quick answers

How long should a wedding highlight film be?

Three to five minutes is the usual sweet spot. A separate full-ceremony edit can be much longer and serves a different purpose.

What export settings should I use?

H.264 MP4 at 1080p with AAC audio is the most compatible default. Make a separate vertical 1080 × 1920 cut for stories and reels.

Can I use a popular song?

For a private film, often practically yes, but public posting can trigger muting or removal. Use a licensed track for anything shared publicly or commercially.
