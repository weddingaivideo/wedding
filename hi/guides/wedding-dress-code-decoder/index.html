Wedding Dress Code Guide

Dress codes describe formality, not colour. When in doubt, the venue, the time of day and the invitation wording tell you more than the label alone.

Last reviewed · 2026-11-15

Open the free tool →

Quick reference

White tieFull evening dress: tailcoat or floor-length gown
Black tieTuxedo or dinner jacket; long or formal cocktail dress
CocktailSuit and tie; knee-length or midi dress
BeachLight fabrics, flat or wedge shoes, no black-tie tailoring

How it works

Read the code with three modifiers: time of day, venue surface and season. A daytime garden 'formal' is not an evening ballroom 'formal'. If the invitation gives no code, match the formality of the venue and avoid white, ivory and anything that competes with the couple's attire.

Regional differences

US invitations often specify 'black tie optional' or 'dressy casual', terms that cause most of the confusion; a tuxedo is safe but not required for optional. In the UK 'morning dress' refers to a specific daytime code for formal weddings, and hats are common for women. Across South Asian wedding events, guests may be expected to wear traditional attire for certain functions and to cover shoulders or heads in a place of worship.

Mistakes to avoid

The reliable mistakes are impractical shoes on grass or sand, wearing white or cream, and treating 'optional' as 'casual'. Two questions are always worth asking the couple: what is the ground like at the venue, and is there a ceremony dress requirement. Those two answers settle most outfit decisions.

Quick answers

Can I wear black to a wedding?

Yes in most Western settings. All-black reads as evening-appropriate; add texture or a colour accent if the ceremony is daytime.

What does black tie optional mean?

A tuxedo is welcome but a dark suit with a tie is fully acceptable. It signals the upper end of formal, not a requirement to rent a tuxedo.

Is it safe to ask the couple?

Always. Ask about the venue floor and any ceremony dress requirement — both are practical questions couples expect to answer.
