Why 5 frames and not a "real" video?

A "real" video renderer in the browser is heavy: ffmpeg.wasm is ~30 MB, takes 10–30 seconds to compile, and pushes browsers hard on phones. Most wedding couples don't need that — they need a clean 5-second motion card they can post today.

Frequently asked questions

Why 1080×1920?

That's Instagram Reels / TikTok / WhatsApp Status / YouTube Shorts at native resolution. It's also fine for older phones — CapCut and iMovie handle it without re-encoding. If you need horizontal 1920×1080, that's a future feature.

Why not just give me the MP4 directly?

Because in-browser MP4 encoding requires either ffmpeg.wasm (≈ 30 MB cold-start cost) or a server round-trip (which would break our no-upload promise). PNG-sequence + your existing video editor is the lightest path that works on every phone. The whole flow is under 3 minutes once you've used CapCut once.

Why PNG and not JPEG?

PNG keeps sharp text edges and crisp lines at 1080×1920. JPEG introduces banding around small white type on dark backgrounds, which is exactly what wedding invites have. File size cost is minor at this resolution — expect ~500 KB to 1 MB per frame.

Can I download more than 5 frames?

इस संस्करण में नहीं. पांच फ़्रेम एक साफ़ 5-सेकंड लूप को कवर करते हैं, जो रीलों/स्टेटस के लिए सबसे उपयोगी लंबाई है। लंबे वीडियो के लिए,एआई वेडिंग वीडियो जेनरेटरगति, संगीत और कैप्शन के साथ 60-90 सेकंड की फिल्में बनाता है।

Does the animation match across frames?

Yes — each template runs the same motion choreography on each frame render. Frame 1 holds the opening pose; frames 2–4 interpolate zoom, text position, or accent sweeps; frame 5 lands on the final composition. They sequence cleanly as a 1-second-each loop in CapCut.
