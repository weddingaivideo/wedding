Vertical Crop Guide for Wedding Photos

A 9:16 crop removes more than half the width of a landscape photo. Choose the subject before you crop, not after.

Last reviewed · 2026-11-15

Open the free tool →

Quick reference

Canvas1080 × 1920 pixels for a standard vertical story or reel
Ratio9:16 vertical; 4:5 is a gentler option for feed posts
Safe zoneKeep faces out of roughly the top and bottom 250 px
SourceKeep the long edge at 1920 px or larger before cropping

How it works

Place the face in the upper-middle third and leave room below for captions, buttons and platform UI. For group shots, crop around the group rather than scaling the whole image down — a wide group squeezed into a vertical frame becomes unreadable. If a photo cannot be cropped without losing someone, use a blurred or colour-matched background and fit the full image inside the frame instead.

Regional differences

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Snapchat all use 9:16 but place interface elements differently, so treat the safe zone as approximate and preview on the actual device. For couples sharing with older relatives on messaging apps, a 4:5 or 1:1 version often survives forwarding better than a full-screen vertical. Check that any text you add is legible in the target language and script direction.

Mistakes to avoid

The common errors are cropping at the original low resolution, cutting through a dress or a joined hand at the frame edge, and putting a caption where the platform puts its own controls. Do not use a zoomed-in crop of a heavily compressed image — vertical video magnifies noise. Export as a high-quality JPEG or PNG, then let the platform compress once.

Quick answers

What size is a 9:16 wedding story?

1080 × 1920 pixels is the standard vertical canvas used by stories, reels and shorts.

How do I keep a group photo in a vertical frame?

Fit the full image with a blurred or colour-matched background, or create a panning crop. Squeezing a wide group into 9:16 rarely works.

Why is text cut off in my story?

Platform UI overlays the top and bottom edges. Keep captions and faces inside a central safe area, roughly 250 px from each end.
